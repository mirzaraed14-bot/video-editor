# fern-inspired — what the creator supplies (and what the build makes)

## The build makes, from your script and a spoken or written scene description
- Dark maps (S3), neon line diagrams (S4), document/web captures with highlights (S5), clean data charts (S6),
  name/date/place tags and slugs (S7), title & chapter cards (S8), a brand sting from YOUR mark (S9).
- Pacing to the measured numbers, the chapter structure, SFX on every reveal, the grade on your footage.
- Prompts / shot lists for anything below that has to be generated or filmed.

## You supply

| # | What | Why it can't be built here | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reenactment footage (S1)** — the 3D people and rooms | Fern uses 3D artists; HTML/vector graphics cannot reach that realism | (a) an AI-video tool you have an account on (Veo / Kling / Runway / Higgsfield): I write the shot prompts per scene, you generate, drop the clips in `projects/<job>/broll/`; (b) a 3D animator from the shot list; (c) stock footage |
| 2 | **Archival clips (S2)** — news, CCTV, photos | sourcing + the fair-use / licensing call is yours | send links; I download (with your OK each time), trim, grade and credit them |
| 3 | **Music bed** | needs a licensed library | your Epidemic / Artlist / Musicbed subscription — pick 2–3 tense cinematic tracks per video, drop them in `audio/` |
| 4 | **Fonts** | the distressed display face and stencil slug face should be properly licensed | approve a free pick (e.g. a condensed grotesk + a grunge texture, a free mono) or buy the ones you like |
| 5 | **Your shoot** (S7) | the "interview" look is lighting and set, not a filter | film darker than your current setup: one warm practical lamp, dark background (the bright pink/neon room fights the look), you off-centre with lead room, camera on a longer lens for shallow depth of field |
| 6 | **Satellite map imagery** (optional) | satellite tiles need a provider account | a Mapbox / Google Maps key; without one, maps are built from open street data (still the Fern teal street-map look) |
| 7 | **Scene descriptions** | the story beats are your call | while scripting, mark each beat: "[MAP: route from X to Y]", "[DIAGRAM: how the scam works]", "[REENACT: him counting cash at 5 AM]", "[DOC: the WSJ article]", or say it on camera and I pick it up from the transcript |
| 8 | **Taste sign-off** | a preset is locked by your review | approve one ~60 s test cut before the preset is used on a full video |
