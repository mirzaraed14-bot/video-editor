# RAW CLIPS: the clipper's rulebook for GTA 6 play (Afterhours, from 19 Nov 2026)

## What this is

The raw lane runs with **zero creative direction from him** (his ruling, 18 Nov: `X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\FEEDBACK.md`).
This file is how the system decides on its own:
- what to clip;
- how to cut it;
- which of his rules apply.

The loop that feeds it is `LAUNCH-MODE.md`.

**The look: Kai Cenat's official Shorts** (`@kaicenatlive`, 16.7M subs; the main `@kaicenat` posts no Shorts).
Measured 18 Nov in `X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\research\streamer-clips-2026-11-18\COPY-SHEET-kai-cenat-shorts.md`. Only 3
clips were measured, and one of them is gameplay, so the rest is UNVERIFIED. The builder's style file is
`projects/gta6-launch/tools/styles/kai.json`. The look:
- **Layout:** face on top (0–960), game below (960–1920), a hard seam, **no separator line**, no zoom moves on the panels.
- **Captions:**
  - 1–2 ALL-CAPS words at a time, hard swaps, about 15–20 swaps per 10 s;
  - a Bebas Neue-class font, cap height 76 px, centred on the seam (y960);
  - white, with a 15 px black stroke and a soft shadow;
  - `!` and `?` allowed.
- **Swears censored** in the captions (`SH*T`) and muted for the word.
- **Effects:** hard cuts only. The energy comes from the caption cadence, plus a white flash or a colour tint on big game
  moments.
- **Ending:** no title bar, logo, CTA, intro or outro. It starts mid-action and stops dead.
- **Length:** median 15 s. Under 10 s and 15–20 s do best on that channel. The payoff lands about 3 s before the end.

**What a viral streamer clip has** is measured in `ANATOMY.md` in the same folder (18 Nov). The summary is in § 2b.

## 1. The gate: what gets clipped

Every candidate window is scored before anything is built. Score it 0–3, one point per part, half points allowed. This is
his FUNNY test from the Scott engine (`presets/youtube-shorts/scott-clary/ENGINE.md`), adapted to gameplay:
- **(a) The reaction:** one beat a stranger reacts to on the picture or his words alone: a laugh, a "no way", a shock.
- **(b) Shown, not told:** it happens on screen. His commentary about something off screen doesn't count.
- **(c) Anyone follows:** someone who has never played gets it within 2 seconds. That means no story, no lore, no
  mission context, which also keeps spoilers out.

**Hard fails**, whatever the score:
- a story cutscene or a plot reveal;
- licensed radio music in the window;
- the moment needs words from before the window to make sense.

**What gets built:** each hour's candidates are ranked. Every one scoring **2.5 or more** is built, plus those at **2.0**
until the hour's cap. The default cap is 5 Shorts an hour; usage moves it (`LAUNCH-MODE.md` § 4).

**Every window that shows a game system** (police/wanted, crime, car theft, shops/robbery, NPCs, animals, money, map,
details) is also logged in `projects/gta6-launch/SYSTEMS-LEDGER.md` for the directed lane, whether or not it becomes a
Short.

## 2b. What a viral streamer clip has (`ANATOMY.md`, 18 Nov)

The sample is small: 4 clips measured in depth (2 of them gameplay), 80 clips by metadata, and 64 sets of top comments.
Read the numbers as a range, not a rule.

**The core:** one event a stranger understands in about 3 seconds with no context. That can be:
- a quotable line;
- a scream;
- an absurd outcome;
- a known person or thing turning up;
- a face breaking.

In 41% of the comment sets, 2 or more of the top 6 comments quote the line.

**What wins at 5M+, gameplay clips by trigger:**

| Trigger | Wins at 5M+ |
|---|---|
| Social absurdity | 8 of 9 |
| Rage | 3 of 3 |
| Hype | 2 of 3 |
| Screams | 2 of 3 |
| Panic | 2 of 4 |
| Self-owns | 2 of 6 |

**What loses:**
- awe and first looks: 0 of 8 at 5M, with a ceiling around 4.5M;
- trailer reactions: 1 of 11;
- opinion talk: 0 of 2.

**So the loading screen and "first look at the city" are weak on their own.** They only work if his reaction is itself
the event: a scream, a line, his face breaking.

**Small channels can win with the moment:** 9 clips at 2M+ came from channels of 100K subs or fewer.

**Moments viewers already knew earn less:** a median of 2.4M, against 12.5M for new moments. GTA 6's day one is mostly new.

**The ranked GTA 6 bits** (an inference, nothing here has seen GTA 6):
1. A scripted game line, then his face reacting.
2. An NPC or the world reacting to something absurd he did, or a physics fail.
3. The first wanted level, a chase or a near-death: screams and panic.
4. A cameo or recognition moment.
5. A self-own on a basic mechanic.
6. Social absurdity: needs other people, so it is UNVERIFIED on day one.
7. A first look at the city.
8. A glitch.
9. The loading screen, or a first car steal on its own.

**The scanner's signals** (S1 to S11, built into `hour_desk`):
- silence, then a burst;
- a voice burst on his own track;
- a game line, then his reaction within 2 s;
- "what if / let me try / watch this" before an impact;
- a name, then an exclamation;
- a cluster of interjections;
- "clip that".

**The trap:** his loudest laugh is often not the payoff. In one measured clip it came 6.5 s before the explosion. So the
payoff is anchored to the visible game hit.

## 3. How a clip is cut

Measured on 4 clips (`ANATOMY.md`), so read these as ranges:
- **Start mid-moment, with the setup in it.** The setup runs 5–18 s. No menus, no title card, no intro, no walking to
  the spot.
- **The payoff lands late:** about 80–90% of the way through the clip (the measured median is about 84%), anchored to
  the visible game hit.
- **End 1.5–3 s after the payoff** with a hard stop. No tail, no "anyway".
- **Length: 10–20 seconds** is typical, from the Kai Cenat measurements. Go longer only when the moment needs the setup.
- **Cut his marker out.** The words "clip that" never appear in a Short, in the audio or the captions.
- **Whole phrases only:** never cut him mid-phrase (`memory/feedback-natural-speech-whole-lines.md`).
- **His timing grammar, when the look uses moves** (`presets/youtube/affan-afterhours-facecam/HUMOR.md`):
  - a slow-down goes on the punch, at exactly 0.8x;
  - the zoom lands on the punch: a static cut zoom, with the setup left at 100;
  - nothing is added that he didn't do;
  - no scripted jokes, ever: his reaction is the comedy (FEEDBACK, 12 Nov).
- **Titles:**
  - 5 words at most, a blunt statement (`memory/feedback-shorts-titles-five-words.md`);
  - true;
  - no spoiler;
  - time words must fit the post date ("day one" only on day one).

## 5. Raw long-forms (automated, after each session)

- **His first hour, as a highlight cut**, in time order, with his commentary and face. About 8–15 minutes, with a
  search-matched title. Evidence: TmarTn's "Grand Theft Auto V - FIRST HOUR OF GAMEPLAY!" has 8.6M (all-time, 2013, a big
  channel).
- **A funny-moments cut** after each session. Evidence: the GTA 5-era funny-moments videos reached 8–10M (Vanoss,
  H2ODelirious, PizzaSlice: all-time, big channels, 2013–2015).
- **The gap, named:** his long-forms lose at the click (`studio-2026-11-15/STUDIO-READ.md`: about 3% clicked). A raw
  long-form lives or dies on its title and thumbnail, so they get the most care in this lane: 3 thumbnail contenders
  from proven formats.
