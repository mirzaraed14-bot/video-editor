## 3. The style on one card (long-form face-cam, current)
| element | spec | source |
|---|---|---|
| length | 5–7 min | results-log |
| pace | 24–33 edit points/min incl. inside nests; first minute ≥ 26/min | CREATOR-HAND § 6 |
| face | ~70–75 % of runtime; face runs median ~10 s | CREATOR-HAND § 6 |
| push | nest → **"110 GTA LF Zoom Preset"** (100→110 across); lone clip → **"105 GTA Lf Zoom Preset"** | CREATOR-HAND § 1 |
| cut zooms | razor + static Scale, median 121–136 % (120–150 normal, ~110 nudge, 160–215 big + reposition, 280–360 extreme ×1–2), hold ~1.3–1.6 s, 4–10/min; ladders 110→120→130→140 on a build | CREATOR-HAND § 1 |
| slow-downs | 0.8x, pitch kept, on jokes/claims only; 0–11 per video — follow the script's jokes | HUMOR.md, CREATOR-HAND § 2 |
| staccato | the video's biggest claim chopped into 3–4 short clips | HUMOR.md |
| overlays | inset ~85 %, rounded corners, drop shadow, over an animated colour matte varied per slot; game footage (Extended Look first) where the line names a thing in the game; screenshots clean (Higgsfield-enhance if soft); ~3–4 runs/min, ~4 s | facecam LESSONS 2026-09-30, CREATOR-HAND § 3 |
| live-source format | screen recording (2560×1440 at 100 %, centre shows) + face PiP at scale 46 with a feathered crop mask, flush top-right/left, no border | CREATOR-HAND § 3 |
| cards | only on a verbatim quote / tweetable rule (Anton-class condensed caps, cream text, ONE hot-pink keyword) | README § 4b |
| meme layer | TV colour-bars glitch 0.2 s at a section change; meme clips 0.5–2 s; Vine boom in the breath after the punch noun (−11…−15 dB gain); shake + bass hit; awkward cricket after a dead joke; **stretch gag** (Scale Width 250–930 %, 0.4–1.5 s, 1–3 per video); swears razored out, not bleeped | CREATOR-HAND § 1, § 4 |
| SFX | pop on every overlay entrance/switch at Volume −6 + Gain −12 (≈ −18 dB); first sound at 0.000 | PLAYBOOK step 6 |
| music | their Epidemic picks (Loungin – Scientific opens; First Born, Brooklyn, Take a Ride), one per section, clip gain −20…−27 dB (~15 LU under voice), **out under every cut zoom**, a silence before section turns | CREATOR-HAND § 5 |
| captions | none on long-form | README § 3 |
| open / close | voice from 0.000 (open on face, an article overlay or a meme); end cold on the face (a cut zoom or a fade to black) | CREATOR-HAND § 6 |
| grade | none; the LED room is the look | facecam PLAYBOOK step 4 |
