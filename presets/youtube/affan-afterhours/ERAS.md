## What the hit run (era 2) did that the recent edits (era 4) don't

Facts, side by side. Causes are NOT established (see the warning above).

| | era 2 (Aug 31 – Sep 12; 10–173k) | era 4 (Sep 23 – 30; 0.3–1.1k) |
|---|---|---|
| game footage | **full frame, sharp, HUD left in**, the main overlay; "show the noun" the instant it is said | **inset 85–95 % over a colour matte** (the creator's rule since 09-30); Collector's Box had no game footage at all |
| face share | ~57–80 % | ~70–75 % (+ PiP) |

| typeset text | Rockstar quotes only (Vice City neon card / white posters), AI-illustrated section cards in list videos | Montserrat/Inter text cards (PC), a quote card + count-up (GI), none (CB) |
| cold open | claim-first or a promise stack with **a picture per promise** (73 % retention at 0:30, results-log) | opens on an overlay or a meme with the voice running |
| comedy layer | slow-downs on punchlines, prop gags, B&W role-play, extreme close-ups | the same + colour bars, Vine booms, memes, cricket, shake, stretch gag |
| SUBSCRIBE bug | Fuel ×2, DIRTIEST ×5 | PC ×3 |
