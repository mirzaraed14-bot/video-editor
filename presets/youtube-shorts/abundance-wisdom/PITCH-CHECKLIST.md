# AW Shorts: the pre-pitch checklist

**Affan, 10 Nov 2026:** "make a checklist of basically everything you have to do before pitching me that particular idea. If it
doesn't pass, there's no use pitching the idea to me. I'm just going to reject it if the source quality is not good and things
like that."

**Affan, 11 Nov 2026** (after a pick that cleared the safety lines but not his viral foundations): "I WANT YOU TO GIVE ME A
DETAILED STATEMENT EVERYTIME YOU GIVE ME AN IDEA THAT THE SHORT HAS PASSED ALL OUR GATES. IT DOESN'T EVEN HAVE TO PASS ALL
GATES IF IT'S INTERESTING ENOUGH TO BUILD, BUT DON'T SLACK."

**Rules:**
- **Every idea arrives with a GATE STATEMENT** (§0 below): every gate, PASS / FAIL / OPEN, with the evidence next to it.
  No idea is sent without one.
- An idea normally goes out only when every line passes.
- **Exception (11 Nov):** an idea with a failed gate may still go out if it's interesting enough to build. The failure is
  named first in the statement with why it's still worth it, never buried or relabelled.
- A fail that isn't worth an exception goes in the kill log (the job's notes or `research/aw-pick-*.md`) with the line it
  failed.
- An honest "nothing passed today" beats a pitch with an open line nobody named.
- The caps guarantee line still follows [feedback-no-false-completion-line]: never typed with a line open.

## The first filter: Instagram proof (Affan, 11 Nov; it comes BEFORE the gates)
"Your first line of defense of filtrating all these ideas … is: has some variant of it gone viral on Instagram … even if it
doesn't pass our gates it's still a very strong contender … our gates are not set in stone, it's just what our experience
has been … high Instagram engagement literally says to your face that this works."
- **Step 1, always:** find the beat (or a variant of it) on Instagram with high engagement, using his beat-mining method
  (creator Reels tabs and outliers; likes and shares written down).
- No Instagram proof makes an idea a weak contender. Strong Instagram proof makes it a strong contender **even if gates
  fail**.
- **The gates below are our experience, not law.** They inform the build and the risks and go into the statement.
- My reading of his words, to confirm: a fail on the **viral-foundation gates** (shape, judgment, G1-G3, freshness)
  doesn't kill an idea with strong Instagram proof.
  - **Facts** (never distort) and **source quality** (his 10 Nov "I'm just going to reject it if the source quality is
    not good") can still kill it.
- **"Proven bits only, stitched" (Affan, 11 Nov):** "I literally do not want completely original ideas … right now … only
  videos that have proven … it will go viral … that's how I got my … birthday band video: I sequenced viral bits,
  stitched them together."

## 0. The gate statement (sent with every idea, 11 Nov)
One table per idea. Each row is a gate, its result, and the evidence: a quote, a number, a link or a file. **No row may say
"✅" without evidence.**

| Gate | What PASS means |
|---|---|
| G1 Quotable image (ENGINE §3) | A line made of nouns and pictures, quoted from the source, not a general description |
| G2 What's on screen | Footage of the thing itself (the event, the scene, the object), not only a talking head describing it |
| G3 Tone | How it's said matches the story (raw, not a bright chat-show laugh) |
| G4 Freshness | Shorts searched in several phrasings, numbers written down. **Since 11 Nov, prior virality of a bit is PROOF, not a kill** (he wants proven bits stitched). The freshness question is whether **our join or sequence** already exists as a Short. Write both numbers down, never hide them |
| G5 Facts | Primary source, counter-evidence, the last 12 months, by ear |
| Judgment | The judgment the viewer holds, **shown in real comments or posts** (a judgment we invent = FAIL), reversed by the person in their own voice |
| Shape | F1-F7, with the birthday mechanics where claimed |
| Source quality | §2 below: the downloaded file, the anchor test, LUFS, music under the voice, minors |
| Proof | Instagram and YouTube numbers for this beat |
| His rules | §5 below |
| **Why it's interesting enough** | One line in plain words: why he'd want to watch it. If this row is weak, nothing else rescues the idea |

Why this exists: on 10 Nov the oxygen chamber, Britney, Dolly, Jermaine and Whitney were all pitched before facts, recent
news or source quality had been checked. Each one then failed a line here.

## 1. Facts (Gate 5): checked first, because it kills fastest
- [ ] Every claim the Short makes, spoken or on a card, traced to a **primary source**: the original upload, a court
      record, a contemporary wire report. Never a narrated "story" channel (FINDINGS §11).
- [ ] **Searched for the counter-evidence** a commenter would post: the person's own camp, later reporting. The oxygen
      chamber died here: UPI 1986 quotes his manager confirming the plan.
- [ ] **Searched the person's last 12 months of news.** Example: Britney's 2026 DUI undercut her 2008 "jail" reversal.
      A recent death, scandal or arrest changes the angle or kills it. Example: Dolly died 25 Aug 2026 and her beat was
      clipped within days.
- [ ] Quotes read from the source transcript, then **heard by ear** before building. Press-reported wording is not enough.

## 2. Source quality: the downloaded file, not the listing
- [ ] **Downloaded** (through `lock.sh youtube`, on his "download them yourself") and probed: real resolution, fps, bitrate.
      "4K" on old TV is usually an upscale. Judge by eye, not the label.
- [ ] **Frames looked at** at every beat window (a contact sheet): sharp enough, nothing burned in that shouldn't be.
      Watch for: competitor logos, other channels' captions or narrators, lower thirds, crude signs, a still over audio.
- [ ] **"Sharp enough" has a reference (10 Nov):** finish a 2 s test with the build's own recipe (the Son ABC recipe:
      9:16 crop to 1080x1920, then Iris 1.0, then Proteus 1x). Compare 1:1 face crops at delivery scale against the
      **softest footage he approved**: the Prince ABC panels (*Why Michael Jackson's Son Had To Step Outside*, about
      25 s and 32 s) and Bieber's Oslo phone shot. Same face region in every tile. At or above Prince = pass. Clearly below
      = kill. Just below = test a zoom-out on blurred fill. Never anchor on the sharpest approved footage (Burnt Hair's
      Katherine). Never trust a single sharpness number across different crops. Example: Whitney's
      `projects/whitney-bobbi-last-day/proof/anchor_compare.jpg`.
- [ ] **Audio measured** for every beat: speech level (LUFS) and noise floor.
  - Fails his first-party HQ rule: far-away or very quiet recordings (example: the UCLA 2009 statement, -47 LUFS, needed
    +26 dB), echo, music under the voice, phase problems.
- [ ] **First-party:** the person speaks on camera themselves, not a third party describing it (MAGIC-FACTOR part 2).
- [ ] **Not a re-cut of a competitor's Short or edit.** Taking footage from another channel's upload of the same beat
      inherits their "seen it" (and the 1 Oct 2026 re-upload rule). Find the original broadcast instead.
- [ ] **No minors on frame** anywhere the beat needs.
- [ ] Plays from Pakistan (no geo-block on the source used).

## 3. Freshness and proof
- [ ] **YouTube Shorts searched** for this beat at this angle (several phrasings), with view counts and publish dates
      written down. A prior Short of 100K+ on the same beat and angle = entry tax; spell out how ours differs, or kill it.
- [ ] **Proof the beat works:** Instagram reels (likes and shares, his beat-mining method) and/or YouTube uploads of the
      same moment, with numbers.
- [ ] His own channel's history checked: was this subject, lane or beat already used, and how did it do (RESULTS.md and
      the Shorts tab)?

## 4. Shape and psychology
- [ ] Shape named (FRAMEWORKS.md F1-F7). For the birthday shape, all of these are present:
  - the viewer's own judgment, said by the person it's about, in the first 3 s;
  - a reversal;
  - a second voice from another era answering it (the join);
  - a picture to end on.
- [ ] The judgment, written as a comment a stranger would type, and installable in 5 s without teaching.
- [ ] Hook, the last line, and why each beat answers the one before. A second half exists (not "a great line with nothing
      behind it").
- [ ] Audience fit: his audience is 52% female, 25-44, US 47%. Sports, WWE and bodybuilding entries get swiped more
      (49-66% stayed).

## 5. His standing rules (the build will apply them; flag any conflict now)
- [ ] Drug and self-harm words: his censor call is asked in the pitch.
- [ ] No music bed (his job). Two subtle subscribe prompts. No end screen.
- [ ] Labels low, captions just under the lips.
- [ ] Title in the AW style: role + verb + reversal + emoji.

## 6. The pitch itself (only after 1-5 pass)
- [ ] Locked prediction with a named biggest failure, plus the 5K and 5M pre-mortems.
- [ ] Cost to build, and what's already on disk.
- [ ] One recommendation, with the evidence for each section above in one line each.
