## Pre-mortems (written before production, 2026-10-19)
**3 · Empty calendar.** Got 5K because: the "habits of 10-figure people" opener sounds like a generic advice riff and gets swiped; the

quotable turn; people send it to their overbooked boss.
**4 · 50 pitches.** Got 5K because: it reads as a TV star talking about himself (Affan's "no lesson" fail); Bravo / NBC pitching is
insider; too many names and numbers too fast for a 17-year-old. Got 5M because: "pitched 50 times, one yes, now on Netflix" is a
universal persistence number with a picture; "what else can we do?" is a question anyone can steal; the Netflix premiere is the receipt.

**5 · Marvel vs RDJ (written 2026-10-21, first views-first pick).** Got 5K because: "Marvel almost didn't cast RDJ" is a story Marvel
fans have seen a hundred times; Cuneo is an unknown older face, so the authority beat is skipped past; the $525M collateral card is too
much reading at Shorts speed. Got 5M because: RDJ's mug shot next to Iron Man is a picture everyone knows; the board member who was
IN the room ("when the lights came on ... do it") is a first-hand angle; it ends on a number ("sold to Disney for $4.5 billion from
bankruptcy") that makes the casting call look like the bet that saved the company.

**6 · War Dogs fake office (written 2026-10-23).** Got 5K because: nobody knows Packouz's face and the movie is 10 years old; the
story needs the viewer to know what a "facility inspection" is; "young girls off Craigslist" reads as cringe and draws the comments.
Got 5M because: "the real War Dogs" is a ready-made curiosity hook (the movie is a cult favourite); a fake office for a $300M Pentagon
deal is a picture everyone gets; "if it wasn't completely illegal, I'd buy you all diamonds" is a quotable line that gets shared.

**7 · Chris Do sneaker pricing (written 2026-10-24, first value-track pick, F6).** Got 5K because: Chris Do is not a face a casual

channel has clipped pricing for years); no comic turn. Got 5M because: everyone has felt "$150 for that? it's fake", so the rule lands
as a memory, not a lecture; it reverses the beginner instinct (cheaper = more clients); $250 -> $800 a day is a concrete number any
freelancer can copy and send to a friend. Freshness (web, 2026-10-24): no prior clip of the sneaker angle found; YouTube unchecked
(Affan to scroll Scott's Shorts tab).
