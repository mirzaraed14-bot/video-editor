   live, and hears him plead "I didn't know". It is the Short's only "physical picture" in the FRAMEWORKS sense. Everything else is
   Speed describing it in 2024.
3. **Proven beats.** The ultimatum lines are proven on YouTube (League Digital `-RYIL98FSTo` 883K, our own `GBvPG_TpMpk` 950K) and on
   Instagram (@gio_palace `DcWIyxyR9Es` 162K likes, @djyoyo `DOcY9D3Aszu` 96K; "mom then vs now" reels at 2.0M and 1.9M views).

## 5. What likely kept it from 1 of 10 (hypotheses, ranked; each names its Studio test)
**H1. We had already told this story, in the same words (a leading suspect; but see § 6: our 950K was itself a re-telling of an 883K).**
- The spine is the same Club Shay Shay lines as our own 950K Short from Dec 2024: "she told me to quit or I'm leaving ... you
  can't do this in my house ... I'm out ... I moved to my dad in Detroit ... Streamer of the Year".
- The same clip was re-cut by League Digital (883K, 29 Nov 2024), and by at least six more Shorts (26K-308K, listed in
  `research/aw-speed-pick-2026-11-13.md` § 2).
- Bolt Motivation's 11M "His Mom Kicked Him Out On Live Stream" (Nitro Camden, with Speed in it) sits on the same words.
- The payoff (the 2026 win) was also out the day before as Speed's own Short (748K by 13 Nov, `research/aw-speed-pick-2026-11-13.md`).
- A Speed fan has very likely seen it, and the feed knows that.
- Test: viewed vs swiped in the first seconds compared with the Son and Siblings, and the returning-viewer share.

**H2. The hook is a statement, not a question or an accusation.**
- "At first, I didn't really have support" is Speed narrating. In FRAMEWORKS, 10 of the 15 winners open on a question and 5 on an
  accusation; none on narration.
- The 368K Ronaldo Short opens on raw emotion and a household name in the first word: "Ronaldo, I love you. I'm so sorry...".
- Test: the 0-3 s retention dip (Studio's retention curve).

**H3. No belief to reverse, and a soft ending.**
- The template (Birthdays, 6M) installs a judgment the viewer already holds ("she won't post = she doesn't care") and flips it with the
  father's own 1993 wound. The kicked-out Short has no such judgment.
- It ends on "she just didn't understand it": warm, but it hurts nobody and asks nothing. That suits likes, not comments or shares.
  21 comments in 2 days against the Ronaldo Short's 245 and Birthdays' 510.
- Test: shares and comments per 1K views against the MJ winners.

**H3, new evidence (found later on 17 Nov, `REPORT.md`):** the Speed family Shorts that went huge took the hard angle we avoided.
- deagzzzshorts `4PMPoZNDSn8` "iShowSpeed's dad deserves all the love for supporting him" (109.3M, 4.1M likes, May 2025): by its comments

  - An advice riff, with no scene and no conflict (VIRAL-BAR D). The arc alone does not carry a Speed Short.

## 7. What a 1-of-10 Speed Short needs (the brief for the new pick)
1. **A beat we have not used and the feed has not worn out.** No Club Shay Shay ultimatum lines, no "one viewer", no three award reveals.
2. **Open on a question, an accusation, or raw emotion on camera**, with a name or a picture a stranger knows in the first 3 s.
3. **A judgment the viewer already holds, reversed by Speed's own voice**: the Birthdays mechanic. The best candidates are a judgment
   people type about Speed ("he's just a loud clown", "he's fake", "he disrespects ...", "he's ungrateful").
4. **A universal stake** (family, a hero, loneliness, money, a promise kept), not an insider prize.
5. **A scene with a live voice** (like the fireworks stream) over telling.
6. **26-38 s, one subject, ending on the sore spot or the warm turn, hard cut.**
