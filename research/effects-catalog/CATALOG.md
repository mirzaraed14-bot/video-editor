## FX-81 · Stepped Low-FPS Motion (added 2026-10-08, the creator's ask)
Built graphics animate at ~20 fps on the 60 fps timeline (each frame held 3x), a film-like step on drifts, pushes and
letter assemblies; real footage stays smooth. Build: `render.sh STEP_FPS=20` (never with MBLUR; grain after the hold).
First used on the SAME NIGHT cold open v2 (g01, g05, g06, g07, the FX-12 title). No reference still yet.
