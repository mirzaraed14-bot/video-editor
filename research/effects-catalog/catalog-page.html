FX-01

Hatched Empire Map

Johnny Harris · watch at 1:38 · Maps & Places

The base map for the whole history: a grey-black satellite relief, graded to warm sepia land and near-black seas, with a heavy vignette and film grain. Each power's territory is filled with a diagonal hatch in its own colour (Russia oxblood red, Iran orange, Britain slate blue, yellow for the ancient 'SUPER POWER'). Territories fade or grow in under a soft edge glow. A single country can be introduced as a glowing pale outline with a small white serif name (IRAN, 0:02 / 0:48). Engraved serif sea names ('Black Sea', 'Caspian Sea') sit on the water, and the camera keeps drifting or pushing slowly. One cut is covered by a warm light-leak flare washing across the map (1:06).

Why it works

It shows who controls what in one glance, and the same palette stays on every scene, so the viewer learns that red means Russia and blue means Britain without being told again.

For SAME NIGHT 0d2a

CH1 2020 Pittsburgh and 2a Prague (glowing outline of the city or country with a small serif name, as a place-establisher before the footage). 0d Olympia announcement (glowing outline of Las Vegas). Use it only where a place matters; the rest of the doc is face-led.

How we'd build it

AE (or HTML/SVG rendered with alpha): desaturated relief basemap graded sepia, seas crushed to near-black, plus vignette and grain. Country shapes come from GeoJSON as shape layers, filled with a 45-degree stripe pattern (Venetian Blinds, or a tiled stripe PNG at about 50% opacity) in faction colours, with a 1–2 px outline and Glow. Animate fill opacity and a mask-feather grow. Outline reveal: Trim Paths on the stroke plus Glow. Add a slow 2.5D camera push of 3–5% per shot. Premiere just places the rendered plates.

FX-02

Octagon Power Badges

Johnny Harris · watch at 9:30 · Portraits & People

Every actor in the story is an octagonal token with a thick white rim and a drop shadow, pinned to the map. Nations show a flag, people show a portrait or crowd photo on a flag backdrop (the Shah's plume breaks out above the frame), and companies show an icon. Under each badge sits a black label box with white condensed caps (RUSSIAN EMPIRE, QAJAR IRAN, IRANIAN PEOPLE). The badges scale up when they are the subject, and a badge that is out of play fades to a ghosted, translucent red (Ottoman Empire, 2:08). A badge can change identity on screen: KING becomes SHAH (8:24 to 8:26), IRAN becomes QAJAR IRAN (1:34), and IRANIAN PEOPLE becomes IRANIAN PARLIAMENT (9:28 to 9:30). During that change the octagon turns into a dashed outline while the photo swaps and the label letters scramble before they resolve.

Why it works

It turns a complicated cast into a small set of game pieces the viewer can track. A label change marks a change in status without needing a new graphic.

For SAME NIGHT 5d

CH1 (badge NICK WALKER and badge MARTIN FITZWATER; 'AMATEUR' scrambles to 'IFBB PRO' as both turn pro). CH4 sponsors as logo badges that ghost out one by one as they drop him. 5d Nick's badge label scrambles to 'MR. OLYMPIA'. It gives the rivalry two fixed tokens to return to.

How we'd build it

AE precomp per badge: octagon shape layer (8-sided polystar) with a white stroke (about 8% of the diameter) and a drop shadow. Flag, portrait or icon goes in a track-matte layer, and the portrait head is cut out and duplicated above the matte so it breaks the frame. The label is a black rectangle with a white condensed sans (Oswald/Bebas class), tracked +20. Pop on with scale 0 to 105 to 100% over 8 frames. Rename: a Text Animator with Character Offset randomize for about 12 frames, while the rim switches to a dashed stroke and the photo crossfades. Ghosting: 35% opacity plus a red tint. Can be made into a MOGRT with a photo slot and a label field.
