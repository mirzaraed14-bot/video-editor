# Demand evidence: YPP Feb-2027 change, Fable 5.2, lead-magnet titles, "Economics of" (2026-11-12)

Method: YouTube search (filter This month / This week / This year) read from the logged-in Chrome in its own tab, nothing played. Sub counts read from each channel's page header via the channel page. Views/age are the numbers YouTube showed at read time. (?) = unverified or looks implausible. Titles truncated. Multiple = views / subs.

## What this says
1. YPP change news is still being published daily (Technical Yogi 98K views at 2 days, 45K at 22 h; Stickman Matt 13.4K at 1 day), but the winners are 2M-sub or Hindi/Urdu-market channels. I did NOT measure August/September, so "has it peaked" is unproven; the only signal is that small-channel uploads this week sit at 12-705 views.
2. Small channels on the YPP topic mostly get under 1x subs (Jet Zhong 3K subs / 221 views, Algomind 3.4K / 127). Best small one is Stickman Matt 8.43K subs / 13,384 views (1.6x). Nobody under 20K shows a breakout on the angle "new rules".
3. Fable 5.2 + faceless/Shorts/money is a live pairing: Danny Why "Claude Fable 5.2 Just Changed Faceless YouTube" 23,869 views at 4 days on 223K subs, Higgsfield "Faceless Channel YouTube Won't Demonetize" 69,928 at 18 h on 526K. Generic AI-general Fable videos from the same sized channels did 60-235K, so the faceless tie-in is not a multiplier on its own. Riley Brown is 312K subs / 81,612 views (the earlier 1.15K reading was wrong).
4. "Give me N minutes / 10,000 hours in N minutes" titles break out for big names (Iman Gadzhi 1.9M, 6.39M subs) but also for mid channels: Dan Zakaria 176K subs 219K views, EnglishYaari 126K subs 862K, KEYFACTS33 39.6K subs 89K (2.2x). Under-50K creator-education examples are rare and weak (Deven Seenath 50.5K subs 21,972; dmscales 23.6K subs 385K on a 300-second "entire algorithm" video is the strongest small-channel proof).
5. "The Economics of ..." repeats beyond one outlier: Stingy 8.96K subs got 162,390 (about 18x) at 3 weeks; The Owners (8.69K) 69,394 on a 747; Millionaire Problems (29.1K) 700,273 on casinos. But the YouTube-creator-economics versions on tiny channels (5-31 subs) did 9-189 views, so the format needs a concrete, surprising subject, not the generic phrase.
6. Caution: many sub counts for micro channels (1-11 subs) are as displayed; treat as (?) where noted.
