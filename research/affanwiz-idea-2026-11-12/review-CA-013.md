# Independent review: CA-013 Druski/Netflix script (2026-11-12)

Web checks done by search snippets only (transcript site 403'd; Forbes, NYT, THR pages not opened). "Unverified" means I could not confirm it, not that it is wrong.

## Defects

1. BLOCKER, title + line 33-37/118 ("Now It's Paying for His Free Shows"; § 1 "Netflix is paying for shows that are free"; "Why would Netflix pay"). Tudum says Netflix "will license select content"; the licence fee and terms are unpublished (the file's own Sources row says "never give a number"). "Paying" asserts a payment exists and is meaningful; it could be nominal. Fix: "licensing" in the title and spoken lines ("Netflix Rejected Druski. Now It's Licensing His Free Shows"), and "why would Netflix license shows that are already free".

2. BLOCKER, § 4 vs § 6 contradiction. § 4: "the free show isn't the product. It's the ad." § 6: Netflix buys the free shows, so they ARE the product. The loop is answered two different ways. Fix: § 4 "the free show works as the ad AND, as it turns out, an asset", or drop the "isn't the product" line.

3. FIX, § 6 loop payoff is stated as Netflix's reasoning ("Because of one sentence..."; "Netflix isn't guessing... Two billion views is the pitch"). No source says why Netflix licensed. Tudum coverage says ownership terms of new shows are undisclosed. Present as the host's read: "Here's what makes it possible / my read". Also "nobody owns them except him" goes beyond the quote ("we" = him and his company); "nobody paid for these except him" ignores the brand money the file itself says funded them.

4. FIX, § 6 "That's what he meant on Diary of a CEO... bidding war". The Sep 24 interview predates the Netflix deal and says "purchase", not license; nothing ties the quote to Netflix. Fix: "Weeks before the deal, he told Diary of a CEO companies now wanted to buy his shows."

5. FIX, § 6 "Same name, same character. Now it's a Netflix series." Sources say Collect & Praise "expands upon" the megachurch pastor skit. "Same name, same character" is unsupported (Mike Epps stars). Fix: "built on a skit he posted for free in January".

6. FIX, § 8 "2017, this was his first skit. The networks said no. So he paid for it himself." Chronology is wrong: the no's came after the 2020 Drake video, and the 2017 skit was not paid for or turned down. Fix: "2017, his first skit. Years of no's later, he paid for it himself and kept it."

7. FIX, relative dates. "last Saturday" (§ 1, § 8) breaks if uploaded after Nov 14. Use "on November 7th" or "this month". "Last month" (Gopuff, Oct 9) breaks if uploaded in December. "This year" for Forbes: the list covers Mar 2025 to Mar 2026; say "on Forbes' 2026 list".

8. FIX, § 3 "So he's spending millions of his own money". The only support is "reportedly up to $3M for one season" (NYT via Complex; I could not find the figure in search, so it is unverified). A reported ceiling for one season is turned into a plain fact. Fix: keep "reportedly" and say "reportedly up to three million a season". Confirm the NYT/Complex wording before filming.

9. FIX, § 4 "Live" paragraph: Coulda Fest, State Farm Arena and the arena tour are labelled REPORTED but spoken with no "reportedly" and no year. Fix: attribute and date them (Net Influencer, Aug 2025), or cut the tour.

10. FIX, § 4 "Brands" paragraph. The "brands took me seriously" quote refers to the 2020 Drake video, not the free YouTube shows, yet it sits under "Here's where free turns into money". Dunkin'/T-Mobile and 38.5M followers: my searches could not confirm them (unverified; the Forbes page was not opened). Check the Forbes entry on screen before filming.

11. FIX, § 1 "Coulda Been Records". Tudum lists "Coulda Been Records Auditions". "You can watch every one of those on YouTube... free": "every" is checked only by showing the Videos tab. Coverage notes it is unclear whether posted sketches stay up. Also "his old shows" implies the whole catalog; Tudum says "select". Fix: "some of his old shows", name Auditions, confirm on the day.

12. FIX, § 2 "For years" / Diary quote. "Netflix told him no. For years." Support: "multiple different projects with Netflix. No, no, no." (years refers to pitching in general). I could not find the exact "It was a no" wording in snippets (Baller Alert/Revolt paraphrase it; one outlet lists Hulu rather than FX). Check the transcript timestamps (01:24:32). Spoken "He pitches TV" vs quote "were supposed to get a show": soften to "deals that fell through".

13. NIT, § 3 "thirty-, forty-minute episodes" and the channel view counts are not in the Sources table. "Two billion views / 220M hours": say "his channel" only if YouTube's post says so; spoken text does not say what the views are of.

14. NIT, § 7 "Not a check for one ad". Terms are undisclosed; say "an equity stake, size not disclosed".

15. NIT, § 8 "The same industry that said no is now booking him" (FX/Amazon/Netflix vs BET/NBC are different companies). "Networks" is loose for Netflix/Amazon. "first digital creator ever" is attributed to "the press": PASS.
