## 2. YPP changes: the "doubling" premise is TRUE for new applicants
Official: YouTube blog, Aug 10, 2026 https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/ (fetched). CONFIRMED (official + vidIQ 2026-08-10 + many outlets).
- Effective Feb 1, 2027. New-applicant entry for ads/Premium revenue: 1,000 subs (unchanged) + EITHER 8,000 public watch hours in 365 days (was 4,000) OR 20M public Shorts views in 90 days (was 10M). Only makes you eligible to apply.
- NEW for everyone, including existing partners: Shorts ad/subscription revenue sharing requires 10M qualified Shorts views in the trailing 90 days. Below that you stay in YPP, keep long-form earnings, Shorts revenue resumes once you cross again. YouTube: "Creators who already earn significant revenue from Shorts are unlikely to be impacted." Promised incentives for sub-threshold channels (Shopping bonuses, brand deals, trend boosts), details "soon".
- Existing partners are not hit by the entry doubling, but must accept new terms in Studio by Jan 31, 2027 (vidIQ, REPORTED). Activity rule per vidIQ: 1,000 watch hours/365d, or 1M Shorts views/90d, or 2 long-form or 5 Shorts uploads per 90 days (REPORTED).
- 500-sub fan-funding tier unchanged (500 subs, 3 uploads in 90 days, 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views); no ads/revenue share. Transition for pending applications on Feb 1 unpublished.
- Also: Premium Lite expanding to all Premium countries; pool 30% Premium / 60% Lite of net sub revenue, creators 55% long-form / 45% Shorts.
- Inauthentic content: renamed from "repetitious content" July 15, 2025 (YouTube said it was always ineligible). July 16, 2026 clarification (TechCrunch 2026-07-20 https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/ ; Tubefilter 2026-07-13) names 3 categories that can't monetize: generic/repetitive/template content (incl. tutorials copying common content), off-putting/distressing content, AI personas of real people on finance/legal/health. Channel-level; Matt Halprin quoted. CONFIRMED. AI used for scripts/thumbnails/editing is not disqualifying (Metricool, REPORTED).
- Shorts bonus ("$300 for 16 Shorts"): REAL but invite-only pilot, not general. Selected YPP creators in US, UK, Germany, Brazil, Japan, South Korea; invitations by email or Studio > Earn > Active bonuses; reported offer: 16 Shorts/month, $300 monthly minimum, up to $2,000/month over 3 months. Individual offers unverified by YouTube; Rene Ritchie presented it on Creator Insider (REPORTED; selfmadesuccess.com 2026-10-23, RouteNote). It is separate from the Feb 1, 2027 10M-views rule (my read: the "Feb 1" in the internal note is likely a conflation with that rule; the 2023 Shorts revenue-share launch was also Feb 1, 2023).
