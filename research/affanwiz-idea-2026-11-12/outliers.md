## Common thread

- Big numbers come from a famous name + a specific dollar sum + a tension word ("trap", "truth", "scandal", "pathetic"), told as a story, not a how-to.
- Tiny-channel breakouts in his own lane are mostly "I tried clipping / Whop for N days, here is what I made" (small real income like $97 or $184 is fine; honesty is the hook).
- YouTube change news ("YouTube changed its algorithm", monetization deadline) pulls 7K to 120K on channels under 10K subs within days.
- Most winners run 6 to 17 minutes (tutorials 5 to 9); the 200K+ ones are 30 to 60 min expose / investigation videos.
- His own 4 hits (Tate, viewbotting, Kiriakou, Penguinz0) match the expose / drama-with-a-lesson pattern; guides and tool reviews are what the data says to avoid.
