## What the numbers say (my read, before outside research)
1. **The ceiling-lifter is an outside audience, not the niche.** Every long-form over 1K has a name or event that people OUTSIDE "Shorts creators" care about
   (Tate, N3on's viewbotting, Kiriakou, Penguinz0) AND a conflict or an insider truth. Praise without conflict (Penguinz0 GOAT 428), a ranking (205) and
   method titles aimed at creators (162, 42) stay small.
2. **Titles addressed to creators cap the reach at the niche.** "Speed's Clippers Always Make This STUPID Mistake" (42) has Speed in it but talks to
   clippers; "Nobody Is Actually Watching These Streamers" (1.3K) talks to anyone who watches streamers. The famous name helps only when the title is
   about the famous person's story, and the lesson stays inside the video.
3. **Guides and how-tos are the floor (15–100)** on a 441-sub channel: search is owned by big channels, and browse has no audience history to match.
4. **Topic hopping:** faceless Shorts → Tate → AI tools → GTA 6 → streamers → Shorts methods. The browse/suggested system has no stable viewer profile
   to serve, so each upload starts cold. The streamer-drama lane is the one that has repeated (1.3K, 444 in 4 days).
5. **Distribution, not only packaging:** at 441 subs, impressions are the bottleneck (no Studio CTR/impressions on file yet: ask for CA-003 and CA-011's
   impressions + CTR + traffic sources; that separates "nobody was shown it" from "people were shown it and didn't click").
