# Older era, notes B — PS5 PRO (08-28, 9.7k) + LEAKER (08-29, 8.1k) + NPC AI (09-08, 350)

Cross-video synthesis of `ps5-pro/`, `leaker/` and `npc-ai/visual-catalogue.md`. PS5 Pro and Leaker are the channel's
first two long-forms, shipped the two days before Fuel System (08-31, 170k); NPC AI went up a week after it, between
RISKIEST (09-05, 35k) and DIRTIEST (09-10, 13.6k). Editor not recorded for any of them. Numbers are the catalogues' own
(scratch runs of `style-probe.py` / `slowmo-scan.py`, a word-timed scratch transcript); they will be re-checked against
the background `probe/` + `words.json` when those land. Notes A covers CRIME SYSTEM + DIRTIEST.

## Constant across the three

- **One room, one locked camera:** `skool` neon above-right, brown leather chair, monitor edge right, medium base with the
  face box ≈ 0.34 of frame height. The voice starts at 0.000; every video ends cold on the face with no end card. No
  captions, no logo bug, **no subscribe animation in any of the three** (Fuel System and DIRTIEST do have it).
- **Show the noun / receipt the claim:** each promise, number, person or source appears on screen the moment it is
  said (motel room for "a photograph on a wall", Garbut's face for "This is Aaron Garbut", the tweet for "45 years",
  GTA V for every "in GTA 5…").
- **Rockstar's words are typeset verbatim, his opinions are not.** Attribution "- ROB NELSON / ROCKSTAR". The **Vice
  City quote card** (blue→magenta panel, inner neon frame, white sentence case, pink accent phrase, pink “ ”, centred,
  grows ~1–2 %/s, hard cuts) is the same template in PS5 Pro and NPC AI — and in CRIME (notes A), where it sits static.
  Cards ship with typos.
- **Insets sit on a blurred version of something** (game plate, or a pure-white page in Leaker) — never sharp game
  footage under a document. Game inserts are full frame, sharp, HUD in.
- **Hard cut is the default**; the only transitions are Leaker's whips/blur-slides and two rack-blur card entrances in
  PS5 Pro. The 0.2-s **colour-bars channel change** appears in PS5 Pro (×2, clean) and NPC AI (×1, VHS-damaged).
- Delivery ~190 words/min with no pause ≥ 1 s (PS5 Pro, NPC AI); Leaker 157/min with breathers under its chapter cards.

## What varies

- **The set and the person** change every era: cyan/sweater/no glasses (08-28) → night/tee (08-29) → coral/sunglasses/
  glove-mic (09-08). PS5 Pro is also the only 60.00-fps export (the rest are 59.94).
- **Face grammar** is the biggest swing: PS5 Pro matches Fuel System number for number (push 1.9 vs 1.8 %/s, snap ×1.30
  vs ×1.30, extremes, broken compositions) — **looks like the same hand cut both**; Leaker barely moves the face; NPC AI
  is nearly static.
- **Overlay language:** PS5 Pro = game footage + Vice City cards + game-branded stat cards (GTA 5 green, RDR2 red) +
  portraits with name tags + the first colour mattes (green `#0B925A`, plum `#8A006A`, a month before CREATOR-HAND's
  09-30 matte rule). Leaker = a **documentary** language that never returned: white-page document insets with drop
  shadows, nine ACT/CHARGE chapter cards sliding in with motion blur, whips, an AI mascot, zero game footage. NPC AI =
  the PS5 Pro cards plus two new text systems (staircase explainer cards, full-width caption-posters in a third pink).
- **Comedy devices:** PS5 Pro — extreme close-ups, card punch-ins (250–300 %), a meme, his own notes as a receipt, a
  release-date slate swap. Leaker — the mascot as a character (walking, jailed), Putin for "Russia". NPC AI — B&W and
  colour shrink-to-black, a mirrored take, a found reaction clip, a self-callback to Fuel System.

## Why NPC AI might read weaker — as an EDIT (not topic, not packaging)

1. **The hook looks backwards and promises little.** It opens on GTA 5 ("pedestrians were furniture"), its first two
   pictures are a B&W face and GTA V, the first GTA 6 gameplay lands at 27.5 s, and the "what's in this video" is two
   items in six seconds. PS5 Pro and both notes-A videos run a ~60–77-s promise stack with a picture per promise and a
   personal stake; NPC AI has neither.
2. **The face has no energy.** Push 0.32 %/s (vs 1.8–1.9 in the two strongest edits), snaps ×1.19 held 1.6 s, nothing
   past ~1.6×. The creator's own later timelines (CREATOR-HAND) put the median snap at 121–136 % with 160–350 %
   extremes; this is the flattest face of any hit-era video measured.
3. **The topic is visual and the edit tells it in type.** NPC behaviour (walking away, the leash, the dropped bag, hiding
   behind cars, persistent memory) is almost never shown: 13 text screens (≈ 77 s of reading) against ≈ 32 s of GTA 6
   footage, and more GTA V than GTA 6 on screen, with two GTA 6 clips recycled from PS5 Pro.
4. **The back third sags.** A 35.6-s uncovered face run (4:41–5:16, with the "10 years ahead of the industry" line that
   PS5 Pro had carded) and a 24.6-s close, about 50 s of visible reading off the monitor, cut rate falling to 5.5 in the
   last minute.
5. **Loose finish.** Repeats kept in ("and and it wraps", "It's literally, it's literally everyone. It's literally
   everyone."), "this is a bit confusing… we'll have to play the game on our own", then a comment CTA — the only one of
   the three not ending on a thesis line.
6. **Three text looks in three pinks** (`#E70C89` cards, `#AB3174` explainer, `#D100F6` caption-posters) — the visual
   system is less coherent than PS5 Pro's single card family.

Its cut count (11.9/min) is the same as Fuel System's (11.3) and its overlay share is normal for the era, so **density
is not the gap** — the gap is a static face, a backwards hook, and telling a visual topic in text. (Title, thumbnail,
timing against the 09-05/09-10 uploads and audience were not analysed here and may matter more.)
