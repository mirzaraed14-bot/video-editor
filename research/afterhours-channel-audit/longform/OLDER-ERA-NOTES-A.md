# Older era, notes A — CRIME SYSTEM (09-03, 10.7k) + DIRTIEST (09-10, 13.6k)

Cross-video synthesis of `crime-system/visual-catalogue.md` and `dirtiest/visual-catalogue.md`. Both were
shipped in the two weeks after Fuel System (08-31, 170k) and before the face-cam preset was written
(09-21). Editor not recorded for either. Numbers below are measured in those catalogues.

## What varies (the editor's choices per script)

- **The quote look.** CRIME: the **neon inset card** — blue→magenta Vice City card, 72–80 % wide,
  centred, untilted, static, rounded geometric sans (white sentence case + pink caps), "- ROB NELSON /
  ROCKSTAR". DIRTIEST: **plain white condensed caps on a blurred, still-playing game plate**, one accent
  phrase in red / blue / cyan / green. Neither tilts or grows its quotes (Fuel System's cards did).
- **Illustration.** DIRTIEST is a nine-item list and gives **every item a full-frame AI title image**
  (chapter cards, 2.2–3.4 s, on the spoken title), plus **AI storyboard panels for the anecdotes** (a
  comic speech bubble, an isometric store map) — 24 AI images. CRIME has none; it uses found art (R★
  card, key art, a character collage, a social infographic, a rival's webcam clip) and the game's UI.
- **New inset kinds in CRIME:** the **HUD magnifier** (the footage's own HUD cropped and blown up on a
  centred panel over the blurred footage, ×3) and the **white page** (a source on pure white with a drop
  shadow, ×2).
- **The past.** CRIME shows "the old world" with GTA V gameplay and GTA V / RDR2 key art; DIRTIEST is GTA 6
  only (RDR2 key art once).
- **Face devices follow the script's jokes.** DIRTIEST (joke-dense): **three 0.8× slow-downs, each fused
  with the biggest cut zoom** (×1.5–1.6), a **+≈10 % push per face run**, ≈ 30 cut zooms (half inside a
  held take, two-rung ladders, an A/B alternation on a rant), a **B&W role-play take with a squash
  distortion**, a screwdriver prop gag. CRIME (argument-led): **no slow-downs, no measurable push**,
  ≈ 17 cut zooms, nearly all on jump cuts.
- **Bugs.** DIRTIEST runs the canned SUBSCRIBE animation **five times** (8.5 s each, never cued); CRIME
  none.
- **First and last frame.** CRIME opens on footage with the voice running and ends on the thesis line;
  DIRTIEST opens on the face and ends on a sign-off ("…and I'm out").

## Against the current preset

- Both confirm **CREATOR-HAND over README** on cut-zoom size (130–160 %, not +25 %) and slow-down speed
  (0.8×, not 0.5–0.6×, and zero when the script has no comic beat).
- Both contradict the README's "~80 % face / overlays are seasoning": this era spends ~40 % of the
  runtime off the face, with no colour-matte insets yet — overlays are full frame.
- Neither uses memes, colour-bars glitches or fades (visual check only; SFX not analysed here).
- Typeset text is rare and earned in both: only Rockstar quotes, a number ("13 YEARS") and — in a list
  video — the section titles.
