## 3. Why one did 6M and the other 142K (same story, same two key lines)

**Read this first: HATED is the baseline, BANNED is the outlier.** HATED's 142K sits in the same band as its late-October MJ
neighbours with different hooks, endings and titles (Dad Couldn't Save 226K, Kids Wouldn't Leave 179K, Told His Sister 118K,
Mother Still CRIES 100K; RESULTS.md, 10 Nov table). So the question is what lifted BANNED, not what sank HATED. What the
evidence suggests (hypotheses from one 6M Short, n=1): **a live argument at the post date** (the birthday week, an interview one week old), **a new join** (an
unclipped 1993 line under a beat the feed had half-seen), and **shares** as the ignition ("shared more than usual", the day-5
break). BANNED's "proven" beat was only a 117K competitor clip, while 7.4M of Instagram proof gave Burnt Hair 83K: **liveness beat
lifetime views.** The hook, ending and title rows below are hypotheses (n=1 each), not findings.

| | BANNED (6.0M) | HATED (142K) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | The accused says the viewer's own belief, first person: "it means I don't love him". The viewer is implicated. | A complaint about trolls: "people lose their minds… told me to kill myself". The viewer is a bystander, plus a self-harm phrase (now a censor rule). |
| Freshness | The JOIN was new: an unclipped 1993 Michael line under a Paris beat the feed had half-seen (117K competitor two days before). | Both core lines had already run on this channel to 6M people. Nothing new joined; the one new beat (2008 cartoons) was cut. |
| Timing | One week after his birthday and one week after the interview: the "Paris didn't post" argument was live. | Late October, no peg. |
| Reveal | At 0:09, in her 2026 voice. | At 0:08, but after a general line about social media. |
| Ending | Loops to the hook's word (Instagram → "my own private way to honor it"): an argument a fan can send. | "That was my best friend": tender, nothing to send. 93 shares on 141K. |
| Title | BANNED: an action with a picture. | HATED: a feeling. |

Shape held (stayed 77.6 % vs 74.3 %, APV ~92 % both): the cut was fine both times. **The difference is the band, set by the
moment: a fresh join + a live argument + a hook that accuses the viewer.**

## 4. The template, as rules for the next pick

1. **A held judgment, said by the accused in first person**, ideally the exact words a commenter would type. Borrowed from a
   clip that is ALREADY travelling (a competitor/fan clip of ≥100K, or an IG reel with a high views-to-followers ratio).
2. **The reveal in ≤4 s, in the same voice**: the cause was the dead man himself (his rule, his wish, his pain).
3. **The why in the dead man's OWN voice, from another decade, with no Short on it.** This is the "bit of our own": low views
   are fine, it must answer beat 2 directly. It is the multiplier and the freshness.
4. **Close the loop on the hook's own word**, ending on a choice of love or a small picture, hard cut. No moral.
5. **Two voices, three sources max.** The second voice answers the first; switch count is not the risk.
6. **A peg** if one exists (anniversary, a fresh interview, a news week).
7. **Title = the correction as a verb on the dead man**: "Michael Jackson BANNED His Own Birthdays". ≤ 5 words in the current rules.
8. **Never re-tell a story the channel already ran** unless the new join is the headline (HATED is the control).
9. Today's extra gates: no real-life minors on frame (BANNED's toddler and family-table photos would fail now), censor
   self-harm words, IG proof per borrowed beat, OURS beats from first-party HQ sources.
