## What would prove or kill the method

- **Working:** after 4-6 Shorts, the mined ones clearly beat the recent non-mined ones of the same age, compared at the same age.
- **Not working:** they land in the same range as recent framework picks.
- **Do not conclude from one Short either way.** The log has been fooled that way many times.
