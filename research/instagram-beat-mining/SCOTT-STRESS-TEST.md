## The score, fixed in advance
**Proof = the highest view count on another channel's Short telling the same story at the same angle, posted BEFORE Scott's upload.** Scott's own Short is excluded. Search per METHOD.md step 8: read `a[href*="/shorts/"]` on desktop results, never `ytm-shorts-lockup-view-model` or the under-4-minutes filter.

**Contamination:** counts are today's, not upload day's. A copy posted after Scott's upload is dropped, and is listed separately because clones of a hit would inflate Bombas. A copy's later growth can't be removed, so state how many scores lean on copies posted within 90 days of Scott's upload.

## The bar
- **PASS only if both hold:** Bombas ranks in the top quarter of the sample, and the median winner's proof is at least 3x the median flat Short's.
- **Anything else: the method doesn't help Scott,** and it says so, without rescuing it with a new definition.
- A separate "subject demand" reading (the best Short on the subject alone, e.g. Bombas, Shark Tank) may be reported, labelled exploratory. It can suggest a forward test and it can't produce a pass.

## What even a pass is worth
One hit of ours. A random score would put Bombas first about 1 time in 12, so a pass is weak evidence and only earns a forward test: log the proof before upload, read at d7 and d14. A fail is stronger evidence than a pass.

Other causes stay open: title, cover, post time, YouTube's distribution (Patrón got 7 views in 6 days, which reads as a distribution problem and not a clip problem).
