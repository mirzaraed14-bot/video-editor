## The shared pattern
1. **One relationship: Michael and his child.** The emotion runs through "father" and "daddy", never through fame, money or conflict.
2. **A wound, then warmth.** First a loss (a dead father, a cruel father), then the turn to love. The last line is a child's raw feeling, not a fact.
3. **The kid speaks for themselves.** Prince or Paris in their own voice carries the payoff. A host's question about "your dad" is a natural hook (Son).
4. **Short and few:** 35-36 s, 5-6 beats. Both times Affan cut toward that: he cut the Dad pitch by 40 %, dropped the grown-up Paris and Prince's tie, and ended on the small child.
5. **The picture is Michael himself** (or Jaafar as Michael). The kids are seen, but the face the viewer comes for is Michael's.
6. **Title = role + verb + 🥹:** "Son Saw His Dad AGAIN", "Became The BEST Dad". It names the relationship and the warm turn.
