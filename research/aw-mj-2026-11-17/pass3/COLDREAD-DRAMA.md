## §2 D1 deep read: "the same man deceived them both"

### 2.1 Is it ONE story?
**Only if the story is a man's word, not two victims.** Cut as "Diana's tragedy, then Michael's tragedy" it is two stories stapled, exactly what A:CHRISTMAS-CUT and the Diana v1.1 cold read killed (R:pass2/coldread-diana.md finding 4). Cut as ONE spine (Bashir's word, spine word LIE/DECEIT) it follows DI #1 and BC ("one antagonist, his own voice, three dates on screen"). **A blocker the first draft of this file had: the contrast needs the PUBLIC half on screen.** "Almost makes me weep" and "never saw any wrongdoing" are both Bashir praising Michael; with no broadcast verdict first, a stranger never hears what he said publicly and "When they lie" pays off nothing. So D1 = the William opener + N1's contrast:
1. William, 2021: the interview was obtained deceitfully [MEM: William's statement of 20 May 2021, video; the quote is DRAMA.md's].
2. Bashir's broadcast verdict, 2003, in his own voice, with no allegation words: "I'd found this easily the most disturbing moment of the past eight months" (the documentary, part 10, 0:25-0:32; BC beat 3, BH H6). NOT on disk (disk has Bashir parts 2, 3, 6 and 16 only, disk-pass lines 74-75); needs a download. The "dangerous place for a vulnerable child" line is the stronger contrast but carries the allegation words (DI flag 1): drop it unless Affan takes the lane.
3. Bashir to Michael, Michael's camera: "it almost makes me weep when I see you with them" (Take Two 36:41-36:59).
4. Bashir, ABC 25 Jun 2009: "I never saw any wrongdoing myself".
5. Button, Michael, Take Two 44:38-44:45: "What hurts you most?" "When they lie. The lies."
Arc in one line (PITCH-CHECKLIST § 4): *the man who got Diana's interview by deceit said Michael was disturbing, said in private he wept at him, and said on the day he died he saw nothing wrong; Michael: "when they lie."* Setup (deceit) -> turn (his two faces) -> ending (a wound). **Strict § 4 test:** "the link between two speakers must be in their own words, not a line I supply." The Diana-to-Michael link is a card unless William names Bashir on camera; if he does, Bashir's name is the link in a speaker's own words and the arc passes. If he does not, the Diana half is "fact A + fact B" and D1 collapses to N1 (one topic: Bashir's word). The link from "When they lie" to Bashir is also ours (the line is general), so it must not be captioned as about him.
