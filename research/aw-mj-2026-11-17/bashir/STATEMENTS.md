## Michael Jackson, 6 Feb 2003 (issued through his London representative)
Sources: AP via newson6.com, 6 Feb 2003 ("Jackson says revealing TV documentary betrayed his trust"); Irish Times, 7 Feb 2003
("Jackson 'betrayed' by Bashir documentary"). The two match word for word on these lines:
- "I trusted Martin Bashir to come into my life and that of my family because I wanted the truth to be told."
- "Martin Bashir persuaded me to trust him, that his would be an honest and fair portrayal of my life and told me that he was 'the
  man that turned Diana's life around.'"
- "Today I feel more betrayed than perhaps ever before; that someone who had got to know my children, my staff and me, whom I let
  into my heart and told the truth, could then sacrifice the trust I placed in him and produce this terrible and unfair programme."
- (not used: "...and that I would never harm any child.")

## Prince William, 20 May 2021 (video statement after the Dyson report)
Source: Yahoo UK, "Prince William's full statement on BBC's Martin Bashir and Princess Diana interview"
(uk.news.yahoo.com/prince-william-full-statement-bbc-113059014.html). The statement does NOT contain "Bashir" or "Martin". Lines:
- "It is welcome that the BBC accepts Lord Dyson's findings in full - which are extremely concerning - that BBC employees:"
- "lied and used fake documents to obtain the interview with my mother;"
- "It is my view that the deceitful way the interview was obtained substantially influenced what my mother said."
- "The interview was a major contribution to making my parents' relationship worse and has since hurt countless others."

## Dyson report facts (20 May 2021)
Press Gazette "Dyson report published"; The Week "'Deceived and induced': inquiry reveals how Martin Bashir landed Princess Diana
interview": Bashir had fake bank statements mocked up and showed them to Earl Spencer; the BBC accepted the findings in full and
apologised (Tim Davie, "full and unconditional apology"); Bashir apologised for the forgery but said it had "no bearing whatsoever on
the personal choice by Princess Diana to take part in the interview."
