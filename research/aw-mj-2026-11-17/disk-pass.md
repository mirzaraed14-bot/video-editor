# Disk pass: accusation -> answer pairs already sitting in transcripts (17 Nov 2026)

Method: every transcript-like file on this PC was loaded (all `words.json`, every `win/asr`, `scan`, `windows`, `sources` json/txt under `projects/`, `research/`, skipping `handoff/`, `.claude/worktrees/` and `hf-graphics/`), flattened to one timestamped corpus (about 76,000 lines, 1,188 distinct sources), then grepped with the B and C pattern lists plus wider judgment-word passes, and each hit was read in context. Disk only: no web, no media opened, no GPU, nothing else written.

How to read the quotes:
- All words are machine readings (WhisperX / faster-whisper). Check against audio before cutting. Seconds are the transcript's clock for that source file (the E: file, not the YouTube player; MJ-IDEAS #3 shows YouTube time can run about 30 s later than the file).
- Times marked `chunk` come from 20-60 s chunk transcripts (the `mj-showed-his-mother/guide/scan` set, Take Two, Oprah, Walters, 60 Minutes): the line is in that chunk, re-time by ear. Times marked `word` are word-level.
- No transcript has speaker labels. Where a host and guest swap quickly (Hotboxin, Osbourne), the speaker is my reading of the text and is flagged.
