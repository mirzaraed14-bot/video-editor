## Lessons for the hub to file
1. A narrated star Short is a ~half-narrator piece built around a filmed scene with real dialogue, and the last line is a real
   voice. Amy's 85 % was the ordinary-people setting. **Correction from the full list (API):** a name in Bolt's title is
   neutral over his whole catalogue, and only in 2026 do hidden names win (1.87M against 698K; a rough name list, a pattern,
   not a cause).
2. **On AW, the face decides, not the format** (API, exact).
   - MJ Shorts since 2 Oct (60 s or less): median ~119K, 6 of 17 at 150K+.
   - The nine non-MJ celebrity Shorts outside bodybuilding: 0 of 9. Bodybuilding's Nick Walker is the exception, at 166.5K.
   - The narrated MJ peers' medians are low: MJ Forever 46.5K, for uploads of 180 s or less over six months. That's not
     directly comparable.
   - A narrated idea has its best chance if it's MJ and it ends in a real voice.
3. **Repeat winners exist on YouTube Shorts:** the same moment keeps winning when retold (Tony × Gaga, 14 retellings at up to
   26.9M; the MJ favela shoot, 8 at 4.25M-40.1M, through Jun 2026). The API search plus the channel-average multiple is a cheap
   way to tell a spent story from a live one.
4. Instagram:
   - Prefer `#name` and a seed page's Reels tab over multi-word keywords.
   - A minimised Chrome window gives a 0x0 viewport (only 3 posts load).
   - After some `/p/` pages, a navigation silently stays put: recreate the tab.
   - Consistent with README, the freezes are Chrome throttling background tabs.
5. **To find own voices with the API, filter out the Shorts:** `videoDuration=medium` plus `videoDefinition=high`. That surfaces
   the long features, talks and local reports where own voices live. Search 4 found a residents' report and a Spike Lee talk
   that a plain search had buried under Shorts.
6. **A hypothesis to track, not a rule: the register matters more than the frame.**
   - In August-September, AW's three tabloid-claim MJ Shorts and its police Short ran 10-18K.
   - Its seven family and wound MJ Shorts ran 77K-6.0M.
   - The police frame alone (5.85M in May, 11.9K in September) is n = 2 and confounded with that split.
