## 1. JUSTIN BIEBER 2013 to 2015

### Verified facts
- 19 May 2013, Billboard Music Awards, MGM Grand, Las Vegas. Bieber accepted the fan-voted Milestone Award and was booed. Sources: CBS News (FETCHED), Christian Science Monitor/AP (FETCHED), Washington Times/AP, TheWrap, Daily Beast (SNIPPET).
- 30 Aug 2015, MTV VMAs (Microsoft Theater, LA). He opened with "Where Are Ü Now", then "What Do You Mean?". Sources: UPI 31 Aug 2015 and Billboard (SNIPPET); UPI 3 Sep 2015 gives the date and venue (FETCHED).
- "Where Are Ü Now" won Best Collaboration the same night (NME, SNIPPET).
- Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, Wednesday 2 Sep 2015. UPI is dated 3 Sep 2015 (FETCHED). NBC Miami and CBS News carry the same interview (SNIPPET and FETCHED).
- Comedy Central Roast taped at Sony Studios, LA, Sat 14 Mar 2015, aired Mon 30 Mar 2015 (NBC News FETCHED for the air date; AJC and Global News SNIPPET for the taping).

### Exact quotes
- 2013 speech, as printed by CSMonitor/AP: "I'm 19 years old; I think I'm doing a pretty good job." Then "This is not a gimmick." Then "I'm an artist and I should be taken seriously and all this other bull should not be spoken." CBS News prints the line as "This is not a gimmick, I'm an artist and I should be taken seriously" and also "It should be about the craft, the craft that I'm making." Mic and Access carry similar fragments. "Taken seriously" is confirmed. The wording after it varies, so quote only the shared core.
- Tracy Morgan (host) is quoted as "Hey, don't be booing anybody here. This ain't no high school." Source: CNN recap via WQAD (SNIPPET only, not read in full). CSMonitor and CBS give no Morgan quote.
- UPI, 3 Sep 2015 (FETCHED): "Last time I was at an award show I was booed." And "I was just feeling judged and just wanting to win so badly." And "I just wanted it so bad." UPI also reports he said he "just put everything on the line."
- NBC Miami (FETCHED): "It was just so overwhelming for me." And "What was so special about the emotional moment was it was authentic, it was real. I wanted it so bad."
- Roast closing, printed in varying forms. NBC News (FETCHED): "through it all, I lost some of my best qualities. For that, I'm sorry." Inquirer (SNIPPET): "I've lost some of my best qualities. For that, I am sorry." AJC (SNIPPET): "I'm a kindhearted person who loves people, and through it all I lost some of my best qualities." Only the "lost some of my best qualities / For that, I'm sorry" core is safe until the Comedy Central video is checked.

### Counter-evidence and gaps
- The booing was a mix of jeers and cheers in CBS's account, not a full-room wall of boos. CBS says he stood at the microphone for a while, appearing confused, before speaking. Do not say "the whole crowd booed".
- Duration of the booing: not stated in any source I read. UNVERIFIED.
- Who presented the Milestone Award: not stated in CBS or CSMonitor. UNVERIFIED. Tracy Morgan hosted the show.
- Jimmy Fallon quote links the 2015 tears to being booed "last time I was at an award show". One snippet (NZ Herald) names the Billboard 2013 show. UPI does not name it. The link is Bieber's own, but nobody I read states which show he meant.
- Tears staged or not: no outlet I found says they were staged. DanceMusicNW (SNIPPET) says he lip-synced part of "Where Are Ü Now" and that Skrillex and Diplo were not mentioned. That is a performance criticism, not a claim about the tears.
- Standing ovation at the VMAs: only the Campanile student paper (SNIPPET) says so. UNVERIFIED by a major outlet. Do not script it.
- "Lifted on wires": outlets describe him "levitating" / flying during "What Do You Mean?" (SNIPPET). The wire detail itself is not confirmed in a quote.
- Skrillex and Diplo on stage with him: UNVERIFIED. The sources say he sang a short acoustic "Where Are Ü Now". I found no source putting them on stage. Wording must not claim they performed live with him.
- Jan 2014 Miami arrest (DUI/drag racing), the egging of a neighbour's house: NOT researched this pass. UNVERIFIED here.

## 2. TONY BENNETT x LADY GAGA 2021

### Verified facts

- 60 Minutes piece aired 3 Oct 2021, 7:50 PM EDT per the CBS story (FETCHED). Follow-up "Anderson Cooper on witnessing Tony Bennett's final act" dated 26 Jun 2022 (CBS, FETCHED).

## 3. MICHAEL JACKSON'S THRILLER DISCLAIMER (1983)

### Verified facts
- Premiere 2 Dec 1983 on MTV, alongside "Making Michael Jackson's Thriller"; a private screening at the Crest Theater, LA on 14 Nov 1983. Source: Wikipedia, citing the Guardian 2013 and a 1983 Courier-Journal review (FETCHED).
- Weeks before the premiere, church leaders told him the video promoted demonology and he could be excommunicated. Jackson ordered Branca to destroy the negatives. The production team locked them in Branca's office. Branca suggested a disclaimer. Source: Wikipedia citing Vanity Fair, Nancy Griffin, Jan 2010, "The 'Thriller' Diaries" (FETCHED); Music Times, Grunge/Vintage News (SNIPPET and FETCHED).
- "About two weeks before the premiere": Grunge/Vintage News (FETCHED). It names no quote.

### Exact quotes
- Disclaimer text: "Due to my strong personal convictions, I wish to stress that this film in no way endorses a belief in the occult." This appears in the Grunge page (FETCHED), which attributes it to the LA Times and says it came from Jackson's Awake! statement. I did NOT see it printed from the video's own frame in a first-party source. UNVERIFIED as an on-screen transcription. Check the opening card against the video.
- Awake!, 22 May 1984, per Wikipedia (FETCHED): he said he "just intended to do a good, fun short film".
- Landis, Billboard (SNIPPET only, page blocked): "We'll put a disclaimer on it." The search summary attributes the line to the Landis/Branca interview but the speaker is UNVERIFIED.
- Landis on set, per Wikipedia (FETCHED): Jackson was "like dealing with a gifted 10-year-old" (Toronto Star 2013) and the response was "a surprise to everyone but Michael" (Guardian 2017).
- Landis at Venice 2017: said the video was made because Jackson "wanted to be a monster" (SCMP/AFP headline, SNIPPET). Reuters quote: "Michael and I always intended it to be seen in a cinema" (SNIPPET). Neither addresses the disclaimer.

### Counter-evidence and gaps
- BELA LUGOSI / Dracula story: NO source found. Wikipedia (read to char 100,000 of 122,849) has no mention. UNVERIFIED. Do not script it.
- Who authored the disclaimer is contested. Wikipedia and Vanity Fair say Branca suggested it. Vintage News/Grunge says Landis and Jackson agreed on it as a compromise. One snippet says Landis wrote it. Say "a disclaimer was added" and attribute the idea to Branca only with "according to Vanity Fair".
- Another detail (SNIPPET): one account says the editor and director hid the canisters. The Branca-office account is the better sourced.
- Branca's own words: no first-person quote from a page I read. Branca on-camera interview: NOT located. Landis on-camera interview on the disclaimer: NOT located.
- 2026 biopic "Michael": Miles Teller plays Branca (JoBlo, Rolling Stone, SNIPPET). I found NO source saying whether the disclaimer or negatives scene appears. UNVERIFIED.
- Jackson left the Witnesses in 1987 per one source (Wikipedia notes a 2001 TV Guide line that he remained one). Conflicting. Avoid.

## 5. BUBBLES THE CHIMP

### Verified facts
- Born 1983 at a primate research facility in Austin, Texas; no exact birthdate (Wikipedia, FETCHED). The sanctuary says it does not know his exact birthdate (CFGA/PR, SNIPPET).
- He grew large and aggressive. Jackson said in 2003's "Living with Michael Jackson" that he had become "pugnacious" (Wikipedia, FETCHED). Fear for newborn Prince Michael II is the stated reason (Wikipedia). Moved to trainer Bob Dunn's ranch, Sylmar, California (2002 or 2003; sources differ).
- Dunn stopped working with great apes in 2004. Bubbles went to the Center for Great Apes, Wauchula, Florida in 2005 (Wikipedia FETCHED, plus SNIPPET sources). Founded by Patti Ragan in 1993.
- Estate continues to pay for his care (Wikipedia citing the CFGA website; Marysue 5 Jun 2026, FETCHED; Fox affiliates SNIPPET). The annual cost figure varies: $17,000 (2009), $25,000, $27,000, over $30,000. Do not use a figure.
- Sanctuary celebrated his 42nd birthday and 20 years there in 2025 (PR Newswire, SNIPPET). A 2026 piece says he is 43 (Hello!, SNIPPET).
- He takes part in painting; Wikipedia says caregivers describe him as sensitive (FETCHED).

### Exact quotes
- Ragan to CNN 2009 (Wikipedia, FETCHED): "They relax. They take naps together." And "They groom each other and they fight and they have arguments, too."
- Ragan in a 2026 piece (Hello!, SNIPPET): "I know Michael wanted to visit Bubbles here, but he was in Europe at the time" and the Fox/Hello wording that he was hoping to come "but that's about the time he passed." Exact wording differs between my snippets, so treat both as paraphrase until read in full.
- Marysue (FETCHED, 5 Jun 2026): the only exact word quoted from Ragan is "anthropoids". The sanctuary calls a suicide rumour "ridiculous" and says it has "given up trying to correct them."
- Dunn (Wikipedia, FETCHED): "Bubbles is an adult chimp and a wild animal. We don't let him out to play."

### Counter-evidence (the "Michael abandoned Bubbles" belief)
- The sanctuary and Ragan say Jackson wanted to visit and died before he could. The Washington Times (2009, via Wikipedia) reported he did not visit, though a representative said he would like to meet him again. Wikipedia also says Jackson regretted the move. Dunn said Bubbles missed Jackson.
- So the evidence is: he handed Bubbles to his trainer, the estate pays, there is no recorded Jackson visit to Florida, and the sanctuary says one was intended. "Abandoned" is the fans' framing. The sources neither prove nor disprove the word. Use "he was moved when he grew too strong and the estate has paid for him since".
- The sanctuary says there are no restrictions on showing Bubbles photos of Jackson or playing his music (Marysue, SNIPPET and FETCHED summary).
- Tabloid claims (Radar) of other reasons are uncorroborated.
- Claim that Bubbles recognised Jackson at his last visit: appears in one snippet attributed to Ragan. Which visit is UNVERIFIED. Do not script it.

### First-party footage located
- Owner: Center for Great Apes (centerforgreatapes.org); the "Living with Michael Jackson" documentary (ITV, 2003) is the 2003 first-party source where Jackson speaks about Bubbles.
- Ragan on-camera interview: none located with an id. Fox 2026 piece ("Where is Michael Jackson's chimp Bubbles? The Florida retirement of a Hollywood legend") is a news video, id unseen.
- HD climax and last line: not located.
