# HD footage inventory + last-12-months news: Michael Jackson and Justin Bieber (as of 14 Nov 2026)

Method: WebSearch only (standard mode) plus two WebFetch calls (Wikipedia "Michael (2026 film)", Hypebeast). No YouTube or Instagram page was opened and nothing was played or downloaded. Search tools return summaries, not page text, so most "HD" calls below are my inference from how the footage was made, not from a frame check.

Legend: **HD** = shot on HD or film and officially remastered, with a source. **SD** = standard-definition origin. **UNSURE** = I could not establish it. "(gen.)" = from my general knowledge, not from a source I retrieved this session. Treat every "(gen.)" claim as unverified.

Honest limits:
- The searches mostly could not confirm the resolution of any specific upload. That has to be checked by `ffprobe` after download, and by looking for upscale softness.
- The MJ interviews and 1980s-90s concerts below are all videotape, so they are SD whatever the upload label says.
- Several of the news hits for late 2026 come from aggregator sites. I flag the ones I could not cross-check.

- Era covered: **1966 to 1988**, from the Jackson 5 through Off the Wall, Thriller, the Victory Tour, and ending with Wembley 1988 on the Bad tour. The 1993 allegations were removed from the film.
- Teaser (6 Nov 2025): 116.2M views in 24 hours, a record for a musical biopic/concert film.
- Sequel: Lionsgate announced on 21 May 2026 that it is in development. Filming late 2026 or early 2027, release late 2027 / early 2028. Jaafar said in Aug 2026 that it will deal with the abuse allegations "from his point of view" (Variety https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/jaafar-jackson-michael-sequel-insight-child-abuse-allegations-39581/ ; theGrio https://thegrio.com/2026/08/22/jaafar-jackson-michael-sequel-abuse-allegations/ ). Fuqua returning is reported by Hot 97 but other reports say not confirmed.
- Official trailers/clips are on Lionsgate/Universal channels. These are HD/4K, but the clips are an actor, not the real Michael, so check whether that fits the "real footage" rule. I did not open any of them. Red-carpet and premiere footage: I found text coverage only (ABC News interview with Prince, Apr 2026, https://abcnews.com/culture/story/prince-jackson-honor-fathers-legacy-highly-anticipated-biopic-132349492 ).
