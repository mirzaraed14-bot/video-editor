- Only the "half a dozen ... cousins or friends" sentence is verbatim-confirmed. The "terminally ill children and their families" / "behind the curtains" wording appears as outlet paraphrase (Espinof, CinemaBlend, Upworthy); exact Guardian wording of that part is UNVERIFIED. Espinof (Spanish): https://www.espinof.com/actores-y-actrices/tom-felton-recuerda-a-alan-rickman-10o-aniversario-su-muerte-cuando-pienso-siempre-sonrio . - Memoir Beyond the Wand (Oct 2022), print: "If anyone understood what a child wanted from a trip to the Harry Potter set, it was him...
