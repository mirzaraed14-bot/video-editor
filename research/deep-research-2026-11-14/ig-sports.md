## Lessons for the hub (I don't write HUB, LESSONS or memory)
1. **Instagram counts can be read without the page rendering:** walk `script[type="application/json"]` for the object whose
   `code` is the shortcode (like_count, comment_count, reshare_count, media_repost_count, taken_at, caption.text). It worked on
   every reel while Chrome was freezing renderers.
2. **Shortcode → post date** (`ig-sports/tools/igdate.py`, media id >> 23 + the IG epoch) dates a whole grid with zero loads.
3. **Broadcaster sites are a second road to first-party HQ when YouTube is flagged:** `yt-dlp -F` on cbsnews.com served 1080p,
   goodmorningamerica.com 720p. Neither is behind the YouTube lock.
4. **Instagram keyword search refuses health, gambling and funeral terms** ("magic johnson hiv", "jordan gambling", "brooke hogan
   funeral", "michael jordan father", "cookie johnson magic"); "<name> story" and "<name> <relation>" phrasings worked.
5. **Shared Instagram pace with six sessions ran 5-10 min per slot,** and tabs froze every ~5 loads (other sessions logged the same).
   A keyword page often won't navigate away, so open the next load in a fresh tab.
6. **Sports storytelling pages are the Instagram seeds in this lane** (@thebucketzco 46.5K followers with 1.6-2.7M outliers;
   @fullpossession; @showtimelakerss). Their outliers are scandal bios (O.J. 2.7M, Hernandez 1.6M, Magic's sex life), useful as
   proof of a judgment to reverse, not as beats to copy.
