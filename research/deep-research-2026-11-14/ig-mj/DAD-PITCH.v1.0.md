## The arc: one word, "Daddy", from the man who wasn't allowed to say it to the children who said it about him

"Daddy, I want to go on the stage": "Go. And do it better than I did it" → his son today: "Awesome" → his daughter, at his memorial:
"Daddy has been the best father you could ever imagine."
**The viewer gets:** the man who couldn't call his father "Daddy" became the father his own children call the best.
**The audience already writes it:** top comment on the 4.2M "Scared Of His Father": "The fact that he gave kids the life he didn't have 😢".
