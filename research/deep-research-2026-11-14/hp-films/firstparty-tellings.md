## Strongest 8 (on-camera adult telling + vivid image), ranked

1. **Radcliffe + Gary Oldman on Rickman knowing Snape's ending** (#1). Return to Hogwarts (HBO Max, 1 Jan 2022). Radcliffe tells Oldman "Just Rickman"; Rickman would answer acting questions with "I'll tell you later". On camera (special), adult, but the special has a score under talk. Backup: Rowling's tweet 18 Jan 2016 "I told Alan what lies behind the word 'always'". https://winteriscoming.net/2022/01/05/harry-potter-alan-rickman-knew-future-severus-snape
2. **Radcliffe on the Seven Potters** (#15). Press-reported 95 takes (record for the series); motion-control camera, he imitated each actor after they filmed it; "I think I got Emma in two takes". Likely video among the junket interviews; print confirmed. https://www.looper.com/494503/this-harry-potter-scene-took-over-90-takes-to-get-right , https://www.cheatsheet.com/news/harry-potter-movies-scene-95-takes.html
3. **Felton on Fiennes' improvised Voldemort hug** (#2). Felton at DragonCon 2011 (video via MuggleNet/Hypable) and again in memoir Beyond the Wand (2022). Take counts conflict: ~25 (DragonCon 2011) vs "one take out of 50" (memoir, per AV Club 19 Oct 2022). Quote (memoir, reported): "To this day, I have no idea why... But I love it!" https://www.avclub.com/harry-potter-tom-felton-voldemort-hug-ralph-fiennes-1849677018 , https://www.hypable.com/tom-felton-speaks-out-about-the-voldemortdraco-hug-at-dragoncon/
4. **Julie Walters, "Not my daughter, you bitch!"** (#12). Reported: "my favourite line... I couldn't believe Mrs Weasley would say that, but it was fabulous to say"; also a DVD special commentary. Empire (2011) print; DVD feature = on camera. https://empireonline.com/movies/news/best-bit-deathly-hallows-part-2
5. **Ralph Fiennes nearly turning down Voldemort** (#22). The Jonathan Ross Show, 16 Mar 2019, on camera, adult. Reported: "Out of ignorance, I just sort of thought, this isn't for me"; sister Martha: "You have to do it!" (nieces/nephew details disagree between outlets, treat as approximate). https://gizmodo.com/ralph-fiennes-almost-turned-down-playing-voldemort-beca-1833357082 , https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/ralph-fiennes-sister-harry-potter/
6. **Rik Mayall cut as Peeves** (#14). 2011 interview (original outlet NOT identified; surfaced by Yahoo UK 9 Jun 2016). Reported: "Er, Rik, we're sorry about this, but you're not in the film. We've cut you out"; "in the film for around three weeks". Unverified whether video. https://uk.news.yahoo.com/rik-mayalls-harry-potter-role-that-no-one-ever-143638534.html
7. **Jason Isaacs inventing Lucius** (#19). Entertainment Weekly 22 Nov 2010 (print) plus Dragon Con / Planet Comicon panels (likely filmed by fans). Reported: asked for a wig; "my wand would come out of the cane" (told to Chris Columbus). Press: pinstripe suit rejected. https://www.the-leaky-cauldron.org/?p=15240 , https://www.the-leaky-cauldron.org/?p=34577
8. **Radcliffe breaking wands by drumming** (#25). Reported as a Hot Ones answer (Dec 2020 by URL date; show name via search summary, UNVERIFIED); later WIRED Autocomplete: really "closer to a dozen, max about 20", denying 60-100. On camera, adult, no music. Note the conflict. https://www.cheatsheet.com/news/daniel-radcliffe-denies-breaking-sixty-wands-on-harry-potter-set-not-that-many.html , https://looper.com/290902/why-daniel-radcliffe-had-to-keep-replacing-his-wands-on-harry-potter

Runners-up: Phelps twins on Fred's death (#8); Matthew Lewis on St Mungo's (#5); Helen McCrory "insurance wouldn't cover pregnant witches" (#13).
