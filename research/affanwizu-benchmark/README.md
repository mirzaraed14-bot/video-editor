# @affanwizu benchmark vs Pakistani creators (2026-11-09)

Pulled 2026-11-09 from his signed-in Instagram (Chrome), read-only: his profile, Instagram's own "similar accounts" list for @affanwizu (80 accounts, all Pakistani; 79 returned), and the first reels page (12) of 33 of them.

**Method (same for everyone):** reels the account itself owns, not pinned, posted 2026-10-09 to 2026-11-06 (UTC, date decoded from the shortcode; the newest 2 days left out as still growing). Plays = Instagram's play count at pull time. Median plays, plays ÷ followers, reels ≥ followers, reels ≥ 100k, likes ÷ plays. His collabs posted from @affanbiz are left out.

**Him:** 44,210 followers (36.4k on 2026-09-18). 19 reels in the window, median 88k plays (2.0x followers), 15/19 above his follower count, 8/19 ≥ 100k, 4 reels 680k-739k, likes 6.1 % of plays. Since 2026-10-12: 16 reels, median 108k, 15/16 above followers. Oct 2-6 median was 20k.

**Result:** 5th of 21 on median plays in the 22k-90k bracket (bracket median 41k); ahead of 5 of the 8 accounts with 2x-3x his followers that were measured. Files: `own_reels.tsv`, `peers.tsv`, `similar_accounts.tsv`.

**Limits:** the list is Instagram's sample of his neighbourhood (comedy, fitness, food, tech mixed in), not the whole Pakistani market; 12 reels per account; one snapshot.
