## 3. "Was it Bieber, the topic or the framing?"
**Bieber has not been tested yet.** That Short broke the birthday Short's mechanics in four places:
1. Its hook was the Oslo walk-off, which already has an **8.2M Short** ("seen it" entry tax, Gate 4).
2. The wound was told by his manager, management and a brain doctor. **Justin's own voice came only in the last 5 seconds**
   (MAGIC-FACTOR part 2: the person says it themselves).
3. The published title, "Painful Upbringing Of Justin Bieber", is a diagnosis with no verb and no reversal. The birthday
   post-mortem found **BANNED** (an action) beat "Traumatized" (a diagnosis).
4. It is one day old (ENGINE §10: "too early"). 6.0K on day one is the non-MJ norm here, and stayed 70.7% says viewers
   didn't swipe it on sight.

The Prince Short ("Saw His Dad AGAIN") is a tender reunion. That is the fist-bump shape, which the birthday post-mortem names as the
control that lacks the wound (it finished ~282K, public). And it is hours old.

## 4. What the birthday Short actually did (the transferable part)
For the record: the ~10M Short is "Michael Jackson's Biggest Misunderstanding" (28 Jun). "MJ BANNED His Own Birthdays" is
**5.9M** (public, today). Both are shape F1, "the wrong belief, corrected by the wound".

**The birthday Short's mechanics, from BIRTHDAY-POSTMORTEM.md:**
1. **The viewer's own judgment, said by the person it's about, in the first 3 seconds:** "If I don't post… it means I hate
   him." No narrator asserts it.
2. **The reveal that the judgment is backwards** (he banned it).
3. **The reason in the dead man's own voice, from another decade.** That join was the fresh part, not the story.
4. **What he gave instead, as a picture.**
5. **Shared as ammunition by a defensive fandom.** Studio flagged "shared more than usual", and the curve went vertical on day 5.
6. **A title with a verb that carries the reversal.**

**Porting it to another celebrity** needs all of 1-3, plus a fandom that defends its star (Beliebers, Swifties, Britney's,
Whitney's). Most WWE and bodybuilding subjects don't have one. **This pass did not find a verified birthday-shape story on
another celebrity:**
- Culkin × MJ is crowded on Shorts (8.1M and 5.5M already).
- Most Culkin footage is of him as a child (minors rule).
- Lisa Marie's "real marriage" angle already ran here (17K).

Finding one is the next research job, not something to fake.
