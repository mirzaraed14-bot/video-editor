September → he dies 29 Oct, 21 days before launch → launch day.
**The viewer gets:** "it's just a game" reversed (it was the thing a dying man kept fighting for); tomorrow isn't promised.
**Evidence:** YouTube Shorts on him 1.50M (Indonesian, 4 Nov), 991K + 991K (Aug), 506K (Le Parisien), 361K, 296K ("He Passed Away
Before Playing GTA 6"), 122K, 64K, 56K (`api/jerry_fort.*`); Kotaku, IBTimes, Dexerto, GTABoom.

channel; the framing is already taken many times (1.5M, 296K); no verified launch-day ending (fans adding "Void" to gamertags is
reported, a Rockstar tribute is not); his footage is phone video from an ICU.
