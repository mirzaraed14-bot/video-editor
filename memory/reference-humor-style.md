**[`presets/youtube/affan-afterhours-facecam/HUMOR.md`](presets/youtube/affan-afterhours-facecam/HUMOR.md)**
holds the creator's comedy grammar, measured off their own finished edit of `gta6-pc-release` (2026-09-22).
Read it before cutting or reviewing a face-cam video.

The short version: **the comedy is timing, not material.** Slow motion marks a punchline and is **always exactly
0.8x** (ten of them in one video, eight on the face, never on a fact); the punch word is isolated onto its own
sub-second clip; the video's biggest claim gets chopped into three or four staccato pieces; and every date,
number and explanation is left completely flat, because that contrast is what makes the slowed moments read.

**Why it matters:** this CORRECTED the face-cam PLAYBOOK, which said 1–3 slow-downs at 0.6x mostly on the
inserts. That was measured off two reference videos and is wrong for the creator's own work.

**How to apply:** the general editing diff from the same pass is in that preset's `LESSONS.md` — notably that
they cut another 10 % out of an already-ruthless rough cut, that a funny aside restating a landed point still
dies, and that they enter sentences later than the rough cut does. Cut-zoom magnitudes are still unmeasured;
Premiere 25.0 hides keyframes in encoded blobs, so read them off the LIVE project or an export instead. See
[[project-gta-documentary-channel]] and [[feedback-creators-hand-is-not-a-bug]].
