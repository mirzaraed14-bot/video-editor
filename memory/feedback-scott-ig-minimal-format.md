Affan, 2026-11-14 (Scott D. Clary channel):
- **YouTube Shorts:** they go up on Instagram in the SAME YouTube format. There is no separate IG variant for them; the PBD urine-prank Short is the first.
- **Scott's own talk on Instagram:** a new strand for his personal stories, motivation, and business or value advice. It is built from what went viral when Scott himself talked, and runs in a SUPER-MINIMAL look:
  - black bars and the footage, with NO zooms and nothing else;
  - one clean main caption font, each line fading in from down to up;
  - ONE single word in a handwritten calligraphy (script) font, appearing BIGGER and BELOW the three-word caption line.

This is NOT the neon-window IG look ([[feedback-instagram-neon-window]]).

**Why:** Affan's read is that Scott's value content can go viral on Instagram in the minimal style popular there, and that our heavier edits suit YouTube better.

**How to apply:** for any Scott IG job of Scott's own talk, use this look, never the neon window, zooms or MG-heavy edits. Pick moments by the topics and frameworks of Scott's own IG hits. The research lives in `projects/_client-scott-clary/research/ig-scott-value/README.md`. Related: [[project-scott-clary-shorts-guarantee]], [[feedback-checklist-shape-is-a-gate]].
