Every email, line or asset shown to Affan goes through a feedback loop first: write many variants, read each one cold
as the prospect would (what do they think, feel, suspect), score them against clear criteria, have a fresh reviewer
attack the best ones, then iterate. Only the winner reaches him, with the reasoning, and with an honest statement of
the effort done and of what could NOT be verified (real reply rates only come from a live test).

**Why:** 2026-11-12 he caught a proposed post-yes question ("who handles your Shorts right now…") that smelled like a
sales setup: "never ever give me something … on which you don't do max effort … don't lie … I care about results".

**How to apply:** no first-draft copy reaches him. State the effort plainly ("N variants, read as 3 host types, one
independent review"), never "max effort" as a slogan. Outreach copy must not trigger the prospect's persuasion radar:
questions are framed around the thing they already agreed to (logistics, their benefit), never vendor-discovery.
Related: [[feedback-sequencing-before-edit]], [[feedback-no-false-completion-line]].

**Sequences too, every channel (Affan, 2026-11-13):** "before providing me a sequence of any sort you have to do your Max
effort... I don't want half baked stuff... the best most in depth research possibility with fable as advisor". The rule
lives in `MAX-EFFORT-GATE.md` at the project root (CLAUDE.md § Rules points at it, so does AW `PITCH-CHECKLIST.md`):
- every beat researched, Instagram included;
- other tellings and voices searched;
- variants compared;
- the advisor (Fable) called before committing and on the final;
- a cold read of the FINAL version;
- the message opens with the max-effort statement, or "NOT max effort yet: <item>".

The MJ Brother Short (11-13) went out without this, and he caught it.
