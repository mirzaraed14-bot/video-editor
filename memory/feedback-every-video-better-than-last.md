Affan, 2026-10-12, as a general rule for ALL videos: "I never want you to stick to just one editing style and lock it in every
single video… let's improve our video [over] the last one, whether it's 10 % or 1 %… constantly make an effort to make our videos
better… we should not stick to just one editing style forever… do your research before making each video."

**Why:** a look that repeats by habit goes stale; the edit is the product, and the only acceptable trend is upward.

**How to apply:**
- Before EACH video: a short style-research pass (the format's references: the creator's own channel, the reference channels, current
  premium examples in that format), and write down in the job's BRIEF what THIS video does better than the last one of its kind.
- "Locked" in a preset means a measured number that must not drift by accident, NOT a ceiling: a deliberate, recorded improvement is
  always allowed (log it in the preset's LESSONS as lesson → change → file).
- Never ship the same build untouched as the previous job; the creator's explicit locks (e.g. [[feedback-split-screen-white-line]],
  [[feedback-caption-punctuation]]) stay, everything else is open to getting better.
