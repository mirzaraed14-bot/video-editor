Affan 2026-11-11 (Scott IG outlier read), after Claude sequenced Scaramucci and then agreed it would not reach 500K: "then why do you even suggest me these brh".

**The rule:** if no candidate clears the bar (for Scott: a realistic ~200K shot, B1 a face strangers know), lead with "this route gives no pick" and the reason. Do not put a below-bar pick in front of him because the brief asked for "the best one or two". A pick with a 5K-60K prediction is not a pick for a 200K goal; it is a bench line.

**Why:** his time is the scarce resource. Every pitch costs him a read and a decision. A pick Claude would not bet on erodes trust in the picks Claude would bet on.

**How to apply:**
- **Score B1 (the famous face) by one question: "would Affan, or a 20-something Shorts viewer, place this face?"** News fame from years ago doesn't count. Claude scored Scaramucci B1 = 1, and Affan had never heard of him.
- **Say plainly when a method fails.** The Instagram outlier read failed as a YouTube pick source, and that failure was the finding.
