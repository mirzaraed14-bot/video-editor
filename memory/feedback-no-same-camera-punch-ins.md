No instant zoom steps on the same camera and no transitions (whips) when the shot hasn't changed. Use one continuous, keyframed
push-in instead; a transition belongs only on a real change of scene/camera/source.

Affan, 2026-10-07 (batch 1 review 2): Chris Do — *"you're just zooming in unnecessarily… the camera would be at Chris and it would just
zoom in again at Chris… I want a constant keyframed zoom in, not an instant zoom in by 25 scale points."* Rollo — *"you just instant
zoom again into his face while the video's already so dynamic… and while he's just talking, the camera is constant, you whip to his camera
again… use the whip transitions or any transition only when there's a change in the scene; if the shot is the same why are you putting
transitions there?"*

**Why:** an unmotivated punch-in or whip reads as an editing tic, not energy, especially in an already dynamic edit.

**How to apply:** this OVERRIDES the QA reviewers' "zoom step ≥ ×1.18 at every same-take joint" and "whip on every cut" rules. Same-camera
joints: keep the framing and let one slow push-in run across them (or cover the joint with B-roll / a graphic / the other speaker);
whips/transitions only where the source or camera changes. Related: [[project-onyx-sample-shorts]].
