Affan, 2026-11-17, after the Scott Clary Reel "He Had 48 Visits Left" (job `projects/_client-scott-clary/48-visits-left/`): the only source of the talk was the podcast mp3 (the feed has no video for that item), so the build put stock b-roll in the DOAC window, 15 pictures in 40 s. His reaction: "why are there so many damn overlays is this an audio only podcast", then "cancel this video ... start researching another topic ASAP", then "no audio podcasts AT ALL AT ALL".

**Why:** a Reel or Short with no footage of the speaker has nothing to show, so it becomes stock pictures over captions, and he rejects that look outright. It also burned a full build on a pick that should have been filtered out at the picking stage.

**How to apply:**
- A pick (Scott's strand, and any channel) is only valid if a VIDEO of the speaker exists and can be obtained: check this at the pick stage, before any research or build time goes into the idea. An audio-only RSS item, a podcast mp3, or a feed with no `podcast:alternateEnclosure` video does not count, however strong the story.
- Put "video source: <where, resolution>" as a gate in every pick, next to the viral-bar evidence ([[feedback-viral-pick-bar]], [[feedback-pitch-checklist]]).
- Do not "solve" a missing picture with stock b-roll, an invented look or an empty window; reject the pick instead ([[feedback-scott-ig-minimal-format]]).
- The cancelled job's audio cut and captions are kept in its folder; RUN.md there is marked CANCELLED.
- The build brief's "hold any one picture no longer than ~4 s" line is what produced 15 pictures in 40 s. The face-cam 4 s rule ([[feedback-face-cam-max-4s]]) is for Shorts with a live speaker, not a licence to fill a Reel with stills.
- When Scott's own talks with video run out, a guest clip with real video, posted with the guest as a collaborator, is the strongest lever in his 2026 IG data (13.3 % 2x vs 3.7 % for his own talks; an untested lead, `research/ig-scott-value/RANKED-BEATS.md`).
- The gate is J in `projects/_onyx-batch3-2026-10/VIRAL-BAR.md`.
