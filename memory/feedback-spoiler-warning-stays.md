When a GTA video or Short opens on his spoken spoiler warning ("Major spoilers for people who haven't completed Chapter 1..."), it **stays in, whole**. It is never collapsed to a chip or a card. Nothing that spoils is on screen while he says it: his face, plus a drawn warning card if a panel needs filling. The gameplay starts on the first tip line.

**Why:** Affan, 2026-11-25, on the C1504 Jason's house Short v1, which had cut the warning for a chip (a hub-brief default): "put my spoiler warning in back brh and no gameplay while i say it the whole purpose is people can scroll of if they havent completed chapter 1". The warning exists so non-finishers can leave before the spoiler. A spoiler picture under it defeats it.

**How to apply:** in sequencing, keep his recorded warning and its bridge line ("So if you have completed, make sure that you do this") in his order, with a spoiler-free picture under them. Override any brief that says to collapse the warning. Logged in presets/youtube/affan-afterhours/LESSONS.md 2026-11-25.
