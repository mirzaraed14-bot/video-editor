Affan's money-guide shape (19 Nov 2026, from his Destiny-economy videos): rank every method by money per minute, "what gets you the most money for as little time". His example: a $1,000 gas station robbery beats a $3,000 mission that takes an hour. His frame for the GTA 6 guide: "how can you get to your first 1m dollars", a pathway in stages 1-4, built on "deep deep research" and "really solid proven material". He put it ahead of six stars because money keeps pulling views for a week or more.

**Why:** a guide is only valuable if its numbers are real, and the efficiency ranking is the hook viewers come for.

**How to apply:**
- Every method carries the HUD cash or bank before and after, plus the time from start to payout, ideally on two runs.
- Teach the rules that shape the order: cash is lost when you're Wasted or Busted (bank it), and some payouts go straight to the bank.
- The game's own pause-map ROBBERIES menu lists targets with a take value and security level; read it before guessing.
- Keep story and mission names out of the pitch.
