Affan, 2026-10-31, reviewing the Pincus IG variant (neon-window look): no more word-by-word caption reveal synced to the speech. Each caption is one solid line of about 4 words that appears whole, with a slight, very subtle bottom-to-top bump, smooth and eased. Everything else about the captions (position, type, colour emphasis, punctuation) stays.

**Why:** our videos now carry many motion graphics, and a word-by-word caption animation on top of them is too much for the viewer to process. Caption animation must be simplified.

**How to apply:** default for every Instagram variant from now on. He framed the reason as true of all the MG-heavy videos, but only named the IG variant, so ask before changing a YouTube version's captions.
