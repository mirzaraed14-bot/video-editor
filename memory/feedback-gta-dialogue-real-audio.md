When a GTA edit holds on a character's line (a quote card / subtitle moment), the hold carries the ACTUAL game audio of that
character speaking, cut from the streamer source at the hold's own source window so it stays in sync. A motion-graphic card
alone over silence reads as "awkward silence" (Affan, 20 Nov 2026, GTA 29 v2: "there has to be the actual game audio of the
actual character speaking"). The card can stay on top.

**Why:** he asked for the game dialogue in the brief; the build kept the old "no streamer audio" rule and filled holds with cards,
and he had to send it back after calling the video otherwise ready.

**How to apply:** the streamer-audio ban still holds for the STREAMER's own voice, chat sounds and profanity; the game
characters' lines are the exception. Check the words against the on-screen subtitle and the streamer's cam (silent), size each
hold to the whole line at plan time, and set the line's MOMENTARY LOUDNESS ~5 LU under his VO as it plays in his mix (his shipped GTA 29 level; not RMS: dense game audio reads 6-12 LU hotter), as a clip level he can ride. A Twitch-muted VOD can often
be replaced by an unmuted clip of the same moment. Tooling: projects/gta6-bigfoot-secrets/work/dialogue/. Related:
[[feedback-streamer-facecams-labelled]], [[feedback-gta6-gameplay-first]], [[feedback-voice-first-mix]].
