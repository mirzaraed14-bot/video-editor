Affan 2026-11-08, on the Herjavec unsaved-spreadsheet Short (angry after three rounds of long titles): "I want that type of title ... 5 words max". His own example: **AI Isn't Your Magic Genie**. No guest name needed, no two-sentence story titles, no "Shark Tank's X Spent 7 Hours..." shapes. He also asked for research before titles, not guesses.

**Why:** the evidence agrees with him. Scott's own channel: "Stop forcing deep connections" 299K, "Stop blaming everyone else" 258K, "Daymond John Almost LOST $250M" 659K, all 4-5 words; the long titles sit at 0.3-5K. The radar's references are all <= 5 words (Cleo "You're Dead. What Next?" 1.1M, Hormozi "Dumb People or Dumb Systems?" 176K). OverseerOS 2026 (18,436 Shorts, observational): 21-40 character titles had a median 53K views vs 9.7K for 1-20 and 11-17K for 41-100.

**How to apply:** give Scott (and other client) Shorts titles of <= 5 words, ~21-40 characters, a blunt statement, command or question; one CAPS word at most; true to what the guest says (a quote or a fair paraphrase, never a false label like "billionaire" for someone who isn't). Offer 5-8 options with one pick, no long explanations.
