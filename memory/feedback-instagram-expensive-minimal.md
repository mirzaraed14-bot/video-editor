For the Onyx Instagram look (`presets/youtube-shorts/onyx-samples/`), batch 2 onward:
- **Big black bars**: a letterboxed, cinematic frame ("Instagram just prefers that cinematic look").
- **Caption animations** (not static captions). Scott D. Clary's channel has ideas; Affan will attach Instagram pages with good
  caption animations.
- **Minimalistic video, a LOT of motion graphics**: minimal, high quality, premium, refined; "the kind you've made for me before but a
  lot more, more refined".
- **Clean, crisp SFX** on the graphics ([[feedback-sfx-on-every-graphic]], [[feedback-premium-sfx]]).
- Overall: "it should look like an expensive video… that expensive, high-quality, minimalistic vibe."
- Before building, research Instagram pages that do this style and learn from them (the PLAYBOOK's "Instagram look keeps evolving").

**Why:** the samples are the sales piece; a premium look is what sells the service on Instagram-first creators.

**How to apply:** every Instagram-look build: letterbox, animated captions, graphics density up, SFX crisp and audible, and a reference
pass in his Chrome first. Related: [[project-onyx-sample-shorts]], [[feedback-caption-punctuation]].
