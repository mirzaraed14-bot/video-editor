Affan, 2026-11-16 (Scott D. Clary, own-talk Instagram strand), about the proposed Reel "Say no to 100 things this month" (to post in mid-November): **"its middle of the month so the ig reel doesn't make sense"**.

**Why:** a clip that says "this month", "this week", "today", "Monday", "this year" or "New Year" reads wrong when it posts on a date that does not match. The viewer hears an instruction that no longer fits their calendar.

**How to apply:**
- Before pitching any Reel or Short, list every time word in the cut and check it against the planned post date (the day after approval plus the build time).
- A time-bound line goes out only on a date it fits (a "this month" challenge at the start of a month, a "Monday" line on a Monday), or not at all.
- Do not trim the time word out of a sentence unless the speech stays natural and whole ([[feedback-natural-speech-whole-lines]]).
