The AW After Effects colour grading (grade stack per section, the faceless look) is NOT a fit for Scott Clary's branding, or
for "any branding in general": it suits faceless videos only. Head tracking / head lock from AW is fine to bring over "if it
makes sense".

**Why:** the AW grade is a stylised look for faceless story Shorts; on a real host/guest podcast it fights the brand.

**How to apply:** when porting AW techniques to client or face-on-camera Shorts (e.g. [[project-onyx-sample-shorts]], the
Scott Clary jobs), take structure and craft (head lock, selection method) but never the AW grade/letterbox look.
