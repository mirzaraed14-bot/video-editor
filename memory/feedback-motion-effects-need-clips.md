A VFX/tracked effect is approved from a short CLIP at real speed of the moments that move most, never from stills.
The Seq 35 head-on-fire (projects/jealous-uncle-fire-head) passed A/B stills ("Looks good to me"), then his Tella review
called it "so unrealistic": it wiggled (an underdamped spring), flame bases snapped between frames, the plume left his
head on turns, and it read as a bonfire "blasting out" instead of natural fire that affects his hair. None of that shows
in a still. Numeric proofs (sync, composite fidelity) only prove fidelity to my own design, not realism.

**Why:** he judges by watching; stills hide wiggle, jitter, lag and attachment.
**How to apply:** before any full render or swap, send a short MP4 of the fastest/most flagged spans (SendUserFile) and
self-check consecutive-frame strips first; never re-cite accuracy numbers as evidence it "looks right". Related:
[[feedback-walkthrough-videos]], [[feedback-fix-only-what-he-named]].
