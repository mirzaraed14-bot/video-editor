In GTA (Affan Afterhours) scripts, don't write "apparently", "allegedly", "reportedly" or "according to X" for facts that are on screen or widely reported (e.g. "smash a window and get in" in the GTA 35 cars script). Say them plainly.

**Why:** Affan, 22 Nov 2026, after reading the GTA 35 cars script: "you try to be like abnormally safe from everything ... All of the things that we discuss are basically confirmed." Hedge words make the VO sound unsure.

**How to apply:** CONFIRMED / REPORTED / UNVERIFIED labels live in the research files only. In the read-aloud script, state confirmed and widely-reported facts flatly; a claim too weak to say flatly is cut (his older rule: no unverified words in scripts).
