When Affan hands over a job and walks away, the video must be BUILT by the time he is back. Questions never block the build: take the stated default for each one, finish and export, then list the defaults taken so he can overrule any of them with a word.

**Why:** gta6-ai-pc-port, 2026-11-10. I finished the speck removal, then stopped with 7 questions and a "sequencing OK?" gate, with a 2:30 am PKT post deadline. He came home at 2:30 am to questions instead of a Short: "bruv wtf i TOLD YOU TO MAKE IT". The build then took about 45 minutes, so it could have been waiting for him.

**How to apply:**
- Older rules say to send the sequencing first ([[feedback-sequencing-before-edit]], the Onyx samples) and to get his OK on a VFX clip ([[feedback-motion-effects-need-clips]]). When he is away or a deadline is close, they become: build v1 on the defaults, send it, and put the questions in the delivery note.
- If there is real doubt, still build. Make the risky piece easy to swap, and say which piece it is.
- A file he never mentioned (like C1454) stays out of the build. Mention it in one line in the delivery note.
