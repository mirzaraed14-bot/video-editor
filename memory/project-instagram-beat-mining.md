Affan, 2026-11-07 (voice, from his phone), on where his growth really came from. He made about $2,600 from YouTube Shorts last
month, up from $600-700 before. The jump came when his Claude subscription lapsed about 3 weeks earlier and he went "back to my
roots" for ideas by hand.

**His method, in his words:** Michael Jackson sad Shorts were going viral, so "what if we make a sad short on Michael Jackson's
daughter". He searched Instagram for, literally, "Paris Jackson sad", scrolled a lot of reels from different creators, saved the
different beats he found, "and then I stitched all of them together. And that's how I saw my 1.3 million views short." That started
the run. He says views are now back to "little to no", and that Claude's "original idea creation is not as good as mine" (he
recalls one of Claude's Shorts reaching about 1.9M, so the bar has been beaten once, not reliably).

**Why:** beats that already went viral are evidence, and our job becomes the sequencing: "we have all the beats technically proven
beforehand ... It's just our job that we sequence them." He wants Claude better than him at this. Caveat to test, not assume:
proven on Instagram is not proven on YouTube Shorts, and a beat many pages have used may be stale. Log each idea's result.

**How to apply:** when picking AW Short ideas, run this search on Instagram Reels (his three main niches: Jackson family, WWE stars,
bodybuilders; Ilia Topuria too, since his exclusive interview came out), in his signed-in Chrome from Claude Code. Read-only on his
account: no likes, saves, follows, comments or DMs. Keep tabs muted ([[feedback-work-in-silence]]). One session at a time at human
pace, stop at any block or captcha (same rule as [[feedback-youtube-sourcing]]). The pick still has to pass
[[feedback-viral-pick-bar]]. Pilot results and the running log: `research/instagram-beat-mining/`. See also
[[project-abundance-shorts-automation]].
