Onyx Influence prospects must look like Scott D. Clary (Success Story), "literally the perfect fit". All five, checked on the channel
page before drafting an outreach email and again before building a sample for a yes:
1. 100K+ YouTube subscribers (established).
2. A real in-studio video set (cameras, lighting); never a Zoom/remote-call podcast.
3. Can clearly afford $1,500+/month (sponsors, a team/producer, a company behind it).
4. Uploads a full episode at least weekly over the last 2-3 months (steady raw material for daily Shorts).
5. Weak Shorts relative to subscribers.

podcasts, Judy Cho uploads too rarely; "they cannot afford my service or they're not uploading enough". He had said this before and
felt it was forgotten. Only Marketing Against the Grain passed.
