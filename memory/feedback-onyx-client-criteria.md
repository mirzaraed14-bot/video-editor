Onyx Influence prospects must look like Scott D. Clary (Success Story), "literally the perfect fit". All five, checked on the channel
page before drafting an outreach email and again before building a sample for a yes:
1. 100K+ YouTube subscribers (established).
2. A real in-studio video set (cameras, lighting); never a Zoom/remote-call podcast.
3. Can clearly afford $1,000+/month (sponsors, a team/producer, a company behind it). The bar follows the price: Affan cut it to
   $1,000/month for 30 Shorts on 2026-11-24 ("I wanna get 3 more clients, the year is ending"), so ~$130K+/yr behind the show.
4. Uploads a full episode at least weekly over the last 2-3 months (steady raw material for daily Shorts).
5. Weak Shorts relative to subscribers.

**Format, not genre (Affan 2026-11-24):** "Scott-like" = the sit-down FORMAT and the budget, never the topic. Any genre
(comedy, entertainment, sports talk are fine) and not only podcasts: any channel where people sit down and talk on camera so
moments can be clipped. Money is the gate he stresses: prospecting is a lot of work, so never chase shows that can't afford him.
Never add a "business fit" filter of my own.

**Why:** on 2026-10-21 Affan dropped three of four batch-4 samples after they were sequenced: Jon Krohn and Stefanie Gass are Zoom
podcasts, Judy Cho uploads too rarely; "they cannot afford my service or they're not uploading enough". He had said this before and
felt it was forgotten. Only Marketing Against the Grain passed.
