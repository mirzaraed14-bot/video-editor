Affan, 2026-11-05, on Owen Hart Wife v1: "your overlay choice is absolutely horrendous ... who is this guy ... use crisp overlays,
highest quality possible ... wrestlers from that era like Undertaker ... they just look shocked. It doesn't have to be Owen Hart, we can
cleverly use some other overlay and make it look like those wrestlers are shocked about Owen Hart." Also: "the past three videos
... haven't really impressed me", and "use Opus for research ... the execution just use Sonnet ... don't cheap out on the credits and
deliver me a poor video ... be efficient, use advisor".

**Why:** literal accuracy lost to impact. I picked footage that was factually from that night (Jarrett, a masked Blue Blazer, a back
of Austin's head, a paparazzi-style arrival photo) and the viewer can't read any of it. An overlay works when it is sharp and when the
face or feeling lands in under a second.

**How to apply:**
- Picking an overlay, ask first: is it crisp, does the viewer know this person, does the emotion read at a glance? The right night is
  a bonus, not a requirement. Someone's shock from another night is fine ("cleverly").
- Grief slots want a memorial-type picture: smiling, warm, grayed. Never a candid or paparazzi frame.
- **The white glow line goes exactly on the picture's edge.** "The whole purpose of the white solid lines is to put it over these changes
  where the image is ending". Crop away a clip's own border, so no dark strip shows between picture and line.
- **A letterbox fills the frame with the blurred background, never black.** A short band's 198 % twin stops short of the frame: scale it to fit.
- Whisper word times run early on drawn-out speech. On a heavily cut speaker, force-align before captions.
- Sourcing: wwe.com video pages serve official clips up to 1080p with plain curl (Owen job `win/wwe_clip.py`). Look for a brand's own site
  when YouTube is blocked.
- Research and decisions in the Opus session (+ advisor), execution through Sonnet agents; spend Higgsfield credits where they show, but
  watch the weekly usage.
