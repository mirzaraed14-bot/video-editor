On every Short (all shows), the speaker's face cam is never on screen for more than about **4 seconds** without an overlay or motion
graphic. Keep a constant rotation of overlays and motion graphics; any line that names a thing (e.g. "contracting officers") gets a
visual. Zooms on the face must be visibly dynamic: quicker and stronger than a slow 1.0 -> 1.07 drift (he called those "very low
intensity"). Pauses are cut YouTube-tight (no audible gaps), but never so hard that speech gets clipped. The hook card's small top
line must be big too (he said "SECURING THE REAL" was too small next to the yellow line).

**Why:** Affan 2026-10-23 on the War Dogs Short: "if the person is there from the face cam for more than four seconds the retention
will drop off so I need like constant rotation of overlays and motion graphics"; "the zooms ... start strong at first but then ... the
zoom is very slow so the short doesn't feel dynamic enough".

**How to apply:** when planning shots, list every face-cam stretch and split any over 4 s with a B shot, card or MG; push-ins of
~10-20 % land within about a second; tighten pauses >= ~0.18 s (measured RMS, 60 ms kept each side) and drop separable fillers
("you know"). Relates to [[feedback-no-same-camera-punch-ins]] (still no instant jump zooms) and [[feedback-head-steady-across-cuts]].
