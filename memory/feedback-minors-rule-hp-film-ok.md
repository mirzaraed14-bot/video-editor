Affan, 10 Nov 2026 (voice), when the HP research killed his Dumbledore vs Voldemort duel idea because Radcliffe was 16-17
on that shoot: "why does it matter if he's considered a minor … in a very safe and sound manner, I don't really see a
problem with that."

**What he ruled:** film footage of the young HP cast (his example: Radcliffe in the *Order of the Phoenix* duel) is OK on
frame for AW Shorts, in a safe, non-sensitive context.

**Claude's default, not his words yet (confirm on the first pitch that uses it):**
- The ruling also covers the films' set and behind-the-scenes footage.
- Press events (e.g. the Aug 2000 casting press conference) are NOT covered until he says so.
- The cast's real-life children and private footage of any child stay out.
- The no-minors rule stays the default everywhere else; he lifts it case by case, as for the Oslo crowd on 8 Nov.

**Why the rule exists:** the repo records no reason.
- The YouTube child-safety angle (comments switched off on videos with minors at risk; 2019 coverage) is Claude's
  inference, not his stated reason.

**How to apply:**
