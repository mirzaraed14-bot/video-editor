A beat goes into a GTA 6 research-led voice-over video (eggs, What If, guides, comparisons) only if it is outlandish and measured HOT: YouTube titles naming it (watcher store), Twitch clip views, Instagram reels. Beats MEASURED warm or cold are out, even to reach the 8:00 floor; a short script gets more hot beats or more depth on the hot ones. A beat nobody has posted yet (unmeasured, e.g. the cellar with 0 titles: an exclusive) may stay only if it is outlandish on its face, labelled "unmeasured".

**Why:** Affan, 19 Nov 2026, after the Bigfoot video: "the Bigfoot point was very good but ... Jason can pick up an iguana like people don't really care about it ... very outlandish stuff that we can cover that is important". The iguana had been labelled warm-cold and still went in as filler. He also asked for the research to be "very thorough".

**Topic bar (same day, later):** the video's TOPIC must be a big question players are genuinely curious about (six stars, the military base, money), "not tiny things people forget in a few hours of gameplay". Measure it by channels covering it, views, hit rate (share of its videos at 100K+), and whether it is still growing on day 2 (a rough signal: older videos slow down whatever the topic). Copy the winning FORMAT from other channels, small channels first ("take notes out of their notepad"). Data: the watcher store (`X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\watcher\data\watcher.sqlite`), written up in `watcher/PITCHES-2026-11-19.md` "FORMATS AND TOPICS".

**How to apply:** label every beat hot / warm / cold from measured numbers before it enters a sequence; cut anything not hot; say in the pitch which beats were cut for heat.
