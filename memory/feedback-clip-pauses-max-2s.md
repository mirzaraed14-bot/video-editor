Inside third-party clips (reporters, Katherine, anyone), "anything over two seconds should be cut down. I like the intentional pauses
but they cannot be more than two seconds, otherwise it just gets uncomfortable." In the same review he ripple-cut ten pauses himself,
including both 1 s holds a locked script asked for and short host gaps (0.4-0.9 s).

**Why:** viewers can't sit through long stretches with nothing said; dead air reads as a broken edit.

**How to apply:** measure every speech-free span from word timings and verify it on the audio level. Cut pauses over 2 s inside clips to
about 1.2-1.5 s with a sync-safe ripple across all tracks, and add a short crossfade on the new joint. Treat crying or applause as
content, but still cut it to 2 s or less. Don't build designed silent holds without asking. Related: [[feedback-cover-phone-reading]].
