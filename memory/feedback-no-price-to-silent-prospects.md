Affan, 2026-11-17, on 11 follow-up drafts to hosts who approved a sample and then went quiet ("$50 a Short, start with ten, pay
only for the ones you post"): "bruv they're not responding why would i give them a price come on man". The 11 drafts were swapped
to the free offer before anything was sent.

**Why:** a price to someone who isn't talking yet reads as a pitch and gives them a reason to stay silent. The first job of a
follow-up is a reply. This overrides the sprint plan's cold-read pick (K1, the paid-test question) and the 11-14 plan's pilot
in follow-ups.

**How to apply:**
- A follow-up to a silent or "thank you" prospect ends on one free, specific question about their show. The current wording is "want
  me to send you the next few moments I'd cut from your recent episodes, each with the line I'd open on?" (K2/K3 in
  onyx-email-formats.md).
- A price goes in only after they reply and engage, or when they ask ("what do you charge?").
- This is about cold or silent threads; a host who asked for the price still gets it straight.
