Affan, 2026-10-21 (18-min Tella review of THE LAST SHOW): "very mid… not cinematic… lacks the fundamentals of a documentary… feels
like a draft… I would not upload this… the whole purpose was to make this video look so premium, expensive… like 20 people worked on it."

**Why:** the plan followed research metrics (0 % synthetic, ≤ 2.5 changes/10 s, freeze instead of slow-mo, near-black cards) instead
of his proven look (the Nick Walker long-form, the FX catalog, gold-matte cards, nested host zooms), and covers were picked by
availability, so the film passed every number and still looked bland and lazy. A missed deadline made it worse.

**How to apply (every Abundance Wisdom long-form):**
- One designed visual per LINE: a Higgsfield image, a real screenshot (Instagram, results sheet), the named person. Never stretch one
  archive clip across lines, and never use an unflattering shot.
- The FX catalog (`research/effects-catalog/CATALOG.md`) and motion graphics are the core of the job; the Nick Walker video is the floor.
- Host: nest consecutive face cuts and give them ONE subtle zoom-in. No per-clip repositioning, no cut zooms.
- No freeze-frame with his VO over it. Opacity fades in and out (no pops); flash transitions on energy turns.
- Level-match inserts to the voice, with short audio fades at joins, BEFORE his first watch. SFX match the action (highlighter → highlighter sound).
- Subtitles on every third-party clip.
- Research numbers are guards, never the target. Ask about the LOOK at checkpoints, not about seconds.

Related: [[project-abundance-wisdom-longform]], [[feedback-cinematic-pacing-longform]], [[feedback-no-same-camera-punch-ins]],
[[feedback-premium-sfx]], [[feedback-usage-budget]].

**Outcome, same day (the redo):** the rebuilt cold open (VO from 0:09 straight off the Prague result, one designed visual
per line: a Higgsfield silhouette among six Olympia trophies, his rebuilt Instagram, a career line ending on RETIRED, the Prague
ledger, a name intro for Martin) got "the whole intro looks so much better… insane". The one miss was a COLLAGE (a raw LED
backdrop + a cut-out torso + a giant word): "hideous, not refined". One subject graded into the film's palette beats stacking.
The redo kit for the next AW long-form: `projects/cbum-last-show/redo-gfx/kit.py` + `work/redo/` (placer, nests, levels, SFX,
verify), brief `work/redo/REDO-PLAN.md`. Never place an A/V item on an audio track (it overwrote V1; lab-notes 2026-10-21).

**His second review (of the redo), same day:** real mistakes were a mock-up Instagram (fake avatar + invented caption: "search
Instagram… recreate the actual post"), static pointers that slid off the men (track them, rest them on the shoulders), an insert
ending mid-syllable straight into his voice ("too abrupt, destroys the immersion": breath + the speaker's room tone), a cover fading
up from black ("cheap"), a 0.37 s flash cover, a push-in on a portrait ("silly"), and one whoosh used 70× (SFX "2/10"). He reviewed
the DAMAGED sequence because its tab stayed open: close a replaced sequence's tab at once. AW LESSONS 23-30.

**After review 2 (same day):**
- Name tags: tag a person only when the identity is PROVEN. He knows these athletes on sight. The Prague tag "Shaun Clarida" survived a
  bib-sheet "match" and was still wrong ("thats not shaun brh"). When in doubt, no tag.
- Subscribe alerts: "little subscribe alerts spread through the video which fit the aesthetic… not too often that it disturbs the
  viewer". Use about 3 per 12 min, small, in the film's own kit, on calm host moments only. His explicit ask overrides the repo's
  default "no CTA" rule.

**Later the same day:** "thats not shaun brh": a man named by elimination (his bib never legible) was wrong. Name only men proven by a

not too often": about 3 per 11 min, 3 s, lower-left in the film's tag style, calm face windows only (AW LESSONS 31-32).
