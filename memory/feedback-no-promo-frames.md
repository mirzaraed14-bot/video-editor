A sourced clip must not flash a promo or end-card frame, not even for 1-2 frames. The case was the Jay Shetty bit in BOTH VERSIONS,
where a "watch now on Spotify and YouTube" frame "makes the video look bad".

**Why:** a stray promo frame reads as sloppy and advertises someone else's channel.

**How to apply:** check every in- and out-point of a sourced clip, plus every internal cut, frame by frame (a contact sheet of ±3
frames). Trim or replace any promo, end-card or subscribe overlay before placing the clip. Related: [[feedback-sourced-stills-must-be-hq]].
