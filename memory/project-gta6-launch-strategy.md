On 19 Nov 2026, about 2.5 hours into his first GTA 6 play, Affan set the launch strategy for Affan Afterhours. He said his own play doesn't help the planned formats: "the research agent will do a better job than me". He also expects the campaign to take 80-100 hours.

**Why:** a faceless VO over streamer footage (Video A, GTA 28) is faster to make. It also covers more of the game than one player can reach. His own footage is only unique for his reactions.

**How to apply:**
- **His play recordings**
  - Use: campaign funny moments (a long-form in his old Urdu funny-gaming style, `presets/youtube/affan-afterhours/FUNNY-MOMENTS-STYLE.md`) plus viral funny Shorts.
  - Bank it as overlays for other videos ("that's going to be actually my footage").
  - Don't push What If capture lists at him.
- **The main formats:** the What If series and guides (money, systems, Easter eggs). Also GTA 5 vs 6 comparisons: graphics, animals, police.
  - These are made research-led, as voice-over videos, like Video A: research and streamer footage → script → he records the VO → edit.
- **How the research agent works (his words, same day):**
  - It collects everything (facts, Easter eggs, guides, What If material, 5 vs 6 points) into topic buckets in `X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\research\vo-slate-2026-11-19\BANK.md`.
  - "whatever it gathers the most we make a video on that".
  - It pings him only when a bucket holds enough for an 8+ min VO; it runs the max-effort gate and writes the script first. Otherwise it keeps working.
  - The session is "Slate GTA 6 voice-over videos: What If, guides, 5 vs 6".
- **State of the loop:** LAUNCH-MODE.md ▶ STATUS. His words: `X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\FEEDBACK.md` 19 Nov.
