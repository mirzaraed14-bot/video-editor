Affan, 10 Nov 2026, on the Radcliffe "drank to stop being watched" HP candidate: "absolutely horrendous choice bruv holy
… idk even know how it passed my checks which are based on the foundations of my viral shorts."

**What went wrong:**
- The idea was ticked on the safety lines (facts, downloaded quality, music under the voice).
- The two lines built on his viral foundations were waved through:
  - **Shape:** "you don't have a right to feel sad" was a judgment Claude invented. Nobody holds it about Radcliffe.
  - **Freshness:** a 2.1M Short and a 3.2M clip on the same beat were called an "entry tax", with his "make them
    better" quoted as cover.
- ENGINE Gates 1-3 never ran:
  - no quotable image (bars and drinking described in general terms);
  - nothing on screen but a studio talking head;
  - a relaxed chat-show tone.

**Why:** the checklist proves an idea is safe to build, not that it can go viral. His winners all have a judgment
the public really holds, reversed by the person themselves, with a physical picture early.

**Affan, 11 Nov:** "I WANT YOU TO GIVE ME A DETAILED STATEMENT EVERYTIME YOU GIVE ME AN IDEA THAT THE SHORT HAS PASSED
ALL OUR GATES. IT DOESN'T EVEN HAVE TO PASS ALL GATES IF IT'S INTERESTING ENOUGH TO BUILD, BUT DON'T SLACK." This is now
§0 of PITCH-CHECKLIST.md.

**Affan, 11 Nov (later the same night):** forget the gates as the first filter. The first line of defence is "has some
variant of it gone viral on Instagram". High IG engagement "literally says to your face that this works". An idea with
strong IG proof is a strong contender even if it fails gates, because the gates are "just what our experience has been
like". (Saved as the top section of PITCH-CHECKLIST.md.)

**How to apply:**
- **Step 1: Instagram proof** (his beat-mining method, likes and shares).
- Then the gate statement as information. A gate fail never kills an idea with strong IG proof on its own.
- Every idea goes out with the §0 gate-statement table: G1-G5, judgment, shape, source, proof, his rules, and "why it's
  interesting enough", each with evidence.
- A failed gate may go out only when it's named first, with why it's still worth building.
- Before ticking §4, run ENGINE Gates 1-3 and show:
  - real comments that hold the judgment (a judgment Claude made up fails);
  - the quotable line made of pictures;
  - footage of the thing itself.
- **Updated 11 Nov:** he wants proven viral bits stitched, so a bit's prior virality is proof. Freshness asks whether our
  join or sequence already exists. Never hide the numbers.
- My reading, to confirm: a fail on a viral-foundation gate doesn't kill an idea with strong IG proof, but facts and
  source quality still can.
