Onyx reaction videos are sent to one prospect, not posted to a channel (only some get uploaded), so they are built FAST. The
standard is one build, one fresh-eyes review and one fix round. After that, accept small residuals that look fine by eye
(e.g. a 20-30 px head shift that reads steady), then deliver. Don't chase measurement thresholds through extra rounds.

**Why:** Affan, 2026-11-14, batch 9 (seven reactions, day two of review loops): "do it quick man these videos shouldnt take this
long these are reaction videos that will be sent to 1 person not even to a channel only some i upload".

**How to apply:** this overrides the "second full review" habit for reactions. Time-box each fix round (~30-40 min). The real
defects still get fixed: a cut word, a desktop flash, captions on lips, an empty stage, unreadable text. P2b goes by eye.
