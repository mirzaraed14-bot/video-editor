# Max-effort gate: before ANY sequence, pick or pitch reaches Affan

**Affan, 13 Nov 2026** (voice, lightly cleaned): "Before providing me a sequence of any sort you have to do your max effort.
I don't want half-baked stuff. Put this in your markdown file, every single time, without any mistakes: that you have done
your max effort, with the best, most in-depth research possible, with Fable as advisor."

**Why:** on 13 Nov the MJ "Brother Saw Him AGAIN" sequence reached him and he said "Yes, build it". He then asked whether it
was max effort, and the honest answer was no:
- Instagram had stopped after 3 searches.
- Three of the four beats had no proof of their own.
- Nobody had searched for other tellings of a line or for other voices.
- There were no variants, and no cold read of the final version.

**Applies to:** every channel and every format (AW, Scott, Onyx, Afterhours, AffanWiz, @affanwizu, any new one). It covers
any sequence, sequencing text, pick list, pitch, beat list or "here's the cut" proposal.

## The gate: every item done before he sees it
1. **Research is complete for EVERY beat, not only the hook.**
   - **Instagram proof (his first filter):**
     - Search for each beat in several phrasings, and write the numbers down.
     - If Instagram is blocked (a 429), the sequence waits until the search can be done, or the message names it first as
       NOT DONE.
   - **YouTube and Shorts proof:** for each beat, in at least 3 phrasings.
   - **Every other telling of each line:** the same story in another interview or from another angle. It may have a
     better picture, cleaner audio, no minors in frame, or a better crop.
   - **Every other relevant voice:** family, inner circle, cast and crew, guests. Search them before settling who speaks
     in the beats.
   - **Our own channel:** check it for repeats.
2. **Every source in the sequence is downloaded, transcribed and read around the line**, so the context of what the speaker
   meant is known. Then:
   - look at the frames;
   - measure the audio;
   - run the channel's quality gate on the downloaded file.
3. **Variants:** compare at least 2 hooks and 2 sequence orders or beat sets, with reasons. The best one goes out, and the
   runner-up is named.
4. **Fable as advisor:** call the advisor (the advisor tool is Fable) twice:
   - before committing to the sequence;
   - again on the final version, before it's sent.
5. **An independent cold read of the FINAL version:** a fresh subagent that judges the files only.
   - Apply every fix it finds.
   - If the fixes were large, run a re-read.
6. **The channel's own checklist still applies:** AW `presets/youtube-shorts/abundance-wisdom/PITCH-CHECKLIST.md`, Scott
   `ENGINE.md`, Onyx `PLAYBOOK.md`.
7. **The sequence message opens with a MAX-EFFORT STATEMENT.** It says:
   - what was searched (counts per platform and per beat);
   - what was downloaded and read;
   - the variants compared;
   - the advisor calls;
   - the cold read;
   - everything NOT done, with the reason.

   Never write "max effort" as a slogan. If any item is open, the first line says **"NOT max effort yet: <item>"**, and the
   sequence is not presented as final.
8. **After it comes the order (Affan, 13 Nov):** the sequence in text, then his OK, and only then the raw build in Premiere.
   A bare "start it" after a research question means the research. If unsure, ask.

## Idea research runs (Affan, 14 Nov 2026): the threshold before ANY list of video ideas reaches him

His words (voice, verbatim): "make sure that you're doing your Max effort in each session make a threshold or like a criteria when
you give me a response I need you to type in something along the lines of that you have done your Max effort as you possibly can
and you have done gone all the way and provided me with the best video ideas possible reference with referenced with other viral
bits and other viral bits from channels and other based on my winners and losers as well".

A research run that hunts for Short ideas (not one sequence) passes this threshold only when EVERY item is done, each with counts and
file paths in its report:
- **A. His winners and losers, read first:** `research/deep-research-2026-11-14/AW-SHORTS-NOW.md` (every AW Short with today's views),
  `X:\Claude Projects\Abundance Wisdom\RESULTS.md`, `research/instagram-beat-mining/RESULTS-TRACKER.md`, the channel `LESSONS.md`.
  Write why the winners won and the losers lost as testable hypotheses, and score every idea against its closest winner AND its
  closest loser.
- **B. Instagram (his first filter):** at least 20 searches (phrasings, hashtags, peer pages) in the lane and at least 40 reels or posts
  read with numbers (views, likes, comments, shares, the account's followers, the date). Every search goes in the shared
  `IG-SEARCH-LOG.md`. A block stops Instagram; the report then says NOT max effort for this item.
- **C. Other channels and other viral bits:** at least 5 peer channels' top Shorts in the lane, and every finalist beat searched on
  YouTube or Shorts in at least 3 phrasings, with numbers. Our own channel is checked for repeats against `AW-SHORTS-NOW.md`.
- **D. Breadth before ranking:** at least 25 candidate beats logged, each rejected one in a kill log with the gate it failed.
- **E. Depth on finalists:**
  - facts verified in 2 independent sources;
  - the first-party source located, downloaded and probed (720p minimum, 1080p wanted), transcribed around the line, its frames looked at, its audio measured;
  - the anchor test against the approved Prince ABC panel;
  - other tellings and other voices searched;
  - the minors rule and censor words checked.
- **F. Shape:** a one-line arc (setup, turn, ending: one story, not two facts), "the viewer gets: ___", and the PITCH-CHECKLIST § 0 gate statement with
  evidence on every gate.
- **G. Variants:** at least 2 hooks and 2 orders per finalist; the runner-up named.
- **H. A locked prediction per finalist** against the right base rate, with a 5K and a 5M pre-mortem.
- **I. The Fable advisor:** before committing to the finalists, and again on the final report.
- **J. An independent cold read of the FINAL report:** a fresh subagent judging the files only. Every fix is applied, with a re-read if the fixes were large.
- **K. The cross-session ledgers:** `CLAIMS.md` checked before any deep dive, so no two sessions chase one beat.

**No quota:** up to 3 ideas that clear the bar, each as a full PITCH (sequence, passing items 1-7 above) or an evidence card. Zero is
allowed: then the report says **"no pick"** first. Filling a slot with a below-bar idea is what produced the rejected Amy Short.

**The statement.** Only when A-K are all done, the report's first line is exactly:
> MAX EFFORT DONE: I have done my max effort as far as I possibly can. I went all the way, and these are the best video ideas I could
> find, referenced against other viral bits, other channels' Shorts and your own winners and losers.

The next line gives the counts: Instagram searches and reels read, YouTube searches, peer channels, candidates logged, finalists
downloaded, advisor calls, cold reads.

If ANY item is open, the first line is **"NOT max effort yet: <the open items>"** and the statement is never typed (memory:
no-false-completion-line).

**One card format for every idea, so the six reports rank against each other:**
`### <rank>. <title, 5 words max> | <lane> | PITCH / CARD`
Each card then carries, in this order:
1. the arc;
2. the viewer gets;
3. a proof table (platform, id or URL, views, likes, shares, followers, date, ratio);
4. the closest AW winner and loser, and why;
5. freshness;
6. the sources (first-party, resolution, audio, path);
7. the gates failed or weak, named FIRST;
8. the locked prediction;
9. flags (minors, censor, length ≤ 60 s).
