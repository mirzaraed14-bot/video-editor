# Max-effort gate: before ANY sequence, pick or pitch reaches Affan

**Affan, 13 Nov 2026** (voice, lightly cleaned): "Before providing me a sequence of any sort you have to do your max effort.
I don't want half-baked stuff. Put this in your markdown file, every single time, without any mistakes: that you have done
your max effort, with the best, most in-depth research possible, with Fable as advisor."

**Why:** on 13 Nov the MJ "Brother Saw Him AGAIN" sequence reached him and he said "Yes, build it". He then asked whether it
was max effort, and the honest answer was no:
- Instagram had stopped after 3 searches.
- Three of the four beats had no proof of their own.
- Nobody had searched for other tellings of a line or for other voices.
- There were no variants, and no cold read of the final version.

**Applies to:** every channel and every format (AW, Scott, Onyx, Afterhours, AffanWiz, @affanwizu, any new one). It covers
any sequence, sequencing text, pick list, pitch, beat list or "here's the cut" proposal.

## The gate: every item done before he sees it
1. **Research is complete for EVERY beat, not only the hook.**
   - **Instagram proof (his first filter):**
     - Search for each beat in several phrasings, and write the numbers down.
     - If Instagram is blocked (a 429), the sequence waits until the search can be done, or the message names it first as
       NOT DONE.
   - **YouTube and Shorts proof:** for each beat, in at least 3 phrasings.
   - **Every other telling of each line:** the same story in another interview or from another angle. It may have a
     better picture, cleaner audio, no minors in frame, or a better crop.
   - **Every other relevant voice:** family, inner circle, cast and crew, guests. Search them before settling who speaks
     in the beats.
   - **Our own channel:** check it for repeats.
2. **Every source in the sequence is downloaded, transcribed and read around the line**, so the context of what the speaker
   meant is known. Then:
   - look at the frames;
   - measure the audio;
   - run the channel's quality gate on the downloaded file.
3. **Variants:** compare at least 2 hooks and 2 sequence orders or beat sets, with reasons. The best one goes out, and the
   runner-up is named.
4. **Fable as advisor:** call the advisor (the advisor tool is Fable) twice:
   - before committing to the sequence;
   - again on the final version, before it's sent.
5. **An independent cold read of the FINAL version:** a fresh subagent that judges the files only.
   - Apply every fix it finds.
   - If the fixes were large, run a re-read.
6. **The channel's own checklist still applies:** AW `presets/youtube-shorts/abundance-wisdom/PITCH-CHECKLIST.md`, Scott
   `ENGINE.md`, Onyx `PLAYBOOK.md`.
7. **The sequence message opens with a MAX-EFFORT STATEMENT.** It says:
   - what was searched (counts per platform and per beat);
   - what was downloaded and read;
   - the variants compared;
   - the advisor calls;
   - the cold read;
   - everything NOT done, with the reason.

   Never write "max effort" as a slogan. If any item is open, the first line says **"NOT max effort yet: <item>"**, and the
   sequence is not presented as final.
8. **After it comes the order (Affan, 13 Nov):** the sequence in text, then his OK, and only then the raw build in Premiere.
   A bare "start it" after a research question means the research. If unsure, ask.
