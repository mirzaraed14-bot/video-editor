# Afterhours ideas: viral video ideas from his play recordings

**His ask (18 Nov 2026):** "every single one-hour recording that I'm going to give you, you are going to be formulating ideas
for my creative videos… for the What If series… but then you're also going to come up with brand new ideas and you're going
to suggest them to me… reference the idea selection… create this skill in yourself to formulate viral video ideas from the
recordings that I make."

**Where this sits:** it is the DIRECTED lane, the videos made with his creative direction after each play session. The RAW
lane (Shorts and long-forms cut automatically from his play) is `presets/youtube/affan-afterhours/RAW-CLIPS.md`. The hourly
loop that feeds both is `presets/youtube/affan-afterhours/LAUNCH-MODE.md`.

**Changed 19 Nov 2026 (his call, ~2.5 h into launch play):**
- His play recordings now feed **campaign funny moments** (the long-form and viral funny Shorts) and an overlay bank.
- The What If series, guides and GTA 5 vs 6 comparisons are **research-led voice-over videos** (FEEDBACK 19 Nov).
- So, per hour: keep only the light ledger of what his footage SHOWS that a research-led video could use as an overlay, and any "bro, imagine if…" he says.
- Don't push What If capture lists at him.

**This skill never builds or scripts.** It finds, scores, banks and pitches. A pitch he picks goes on to a sequence, his OK
and only then a build. The judgment runs in the hub (Opus) with the Fable advisor, and Sonnet helpers only gather.

## Every hour (after the hour desk has run)

**Inputs** (in `projects/gta6-launch/H<NN>/`):
- the transcript of his voice;
- `candidates.json`, `CANDIDATES.md` and the contact sheets;
- `projects/gta6-launch/SYSTEMS-LEDGER.md`.

1. **The What If ledger.** For each episode in the series file (arrest, car theft, store robbery, punching an NPC, 6 stars,
   and the rest), record:
   - which of its tests he did this hour, with timestamps;
   - what happened (CONFIRMED: seen in his footage);
   - what is still missing.

   Write a **"test next session" list** of at most 5 concrete actions. That is capture guidance, not creative direction.

   **The IGN review test list** (`X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\launch-ideas\IGN-REVIEW-TEST-LIST.md`, 18 Nov):
   - Every candidate in `candidates.json` that carries signal **S12** is him testing one of the reviewer's claims. Its
     `detail` holds the words after "IGN check".
   - Mark each claim on the list as one of:
     - CONFIRMED, with its timestamp;
     - NOT AS CLAIMED, saying what he saw instead;
     - UNCLEAR.
   - "The reviewer said X; here it is, or isn't" is a discovery for step 2. Freshness still has to be checked.
2. **Discoveries.** Anything in the hour that is a fundamental change from GTA 5, or a surprise he reacted to, becomes a
   candidate idea. That includes his own off-hand thoughts said out loud ("bro, imagine if…").
3. **Score each idea.** One line per field:
   - **Working title:** search-matched, written first.
   - **The viewer gets: ___**
   - **The fundamental change it stands on**, with the timestamp in his footage that proves it.
   - **The shape**, and its measured precedent (title, views, channel; from the files above, labelled).
   - **The repeat check** against `REPEAT-INDEX.md` and his uploads: NEW, PARTIAL (what's new) or REPEAT. A REPEAT is out.
   - **Freshness:** what others have already posted, from the watcher's `FINDS.md` if it is on; otherwise UNCHECKED.
   - **Footage:** what exists (ledger references), and what he still has to record.
   - **Does it pass his bar?** Yes or no, in one clause, and whether he'd be interested or pretending.
4. **Bank it.** Every idea goes into `X:\Claude Projects\GTA 6\launch-ideas\IDEAS.md`, ranked, with the hour it came
   from. Nothing is deleted; ideas are merged or retired with a reason.
5. **Tell him in one line in the hour report:** how many new ideas were banked, the strongest so far, and that it is a
   **lead, not yet max-effort**. If nothing cleared his bar, say "no new idea this hour" once.

## At the end of each play session: the pitch

When he stops playing (the session that the directed videos come from):
1. Take the top 2–3 banked ideas.
2. Run the full max-effort gate on them (`MAX-EFFORT-GATE.md`):
   - Instagram and YouTube proof in several phrasings;
   - every other telling;
   - our own channel;
   - variants;
   - the Fable advisor twice;
   - an independent cold read.
3. Pitch them, opening with the statement of what was done.
4. Lead with what is measured as working for channels his size **this week**.
5. If none clears his bar, say "no pick" first (`memory/feedback-no-below-bar-pitches.md`).
6. The one he picks becomes a sequence for his OK, then the edit.

## Never

- Invent a fact, a statistic or a personal story. Label everything CONFIRMED (seen in his footage), REPORTED, UNVERIFIED
  or ESTIMATE.
- Pitch a story spoiler as a title or a thumbnail.
- Write jokes or lines for him. His reaction is the comedy.
- Pitch below his bar, or build anything from this skill.
