## 2. The premium treatment, beat by beat

**The glue (whole film)**
- **One grain + texture layer over everything** (host and archive), so mixed-quality sources read as one film.
- **Era treatment:** 2020 (1a) and 2022 (1b–1d, 5h) get a quiet "past" finish (slightly desaturated, film texture,
  soft gate weave); 2024–26 stay clean. The time jumps become readable without a word.
- **The evidence tag:** one small, consistent tag on every clip, `SOURCE · DATE` (the script's credibility deposit:
  "the real person, on camera, with the date"). Taught once in the cold open, then it's on every clip.
- **Type system:** one serif for dates, chapter titles and pull-quotes (documentary weight), one sans for labels and
  tags. Nothing else.
- **Sound:** an audible Epidemic SFX on every graphic entrance (the standing rule), room tone under stills, a sub hit on
  date cards, a riser into each chapter card, and SILENCE where the script asks (5d: 8 s, the close: no swell).
- **Same-camera rule:** no punch-in or whip on a host-to-host joint; one slow keyed push-in per host run (as in LF1).
